## Judged demo artefacts, not findings
Buddy says "130K to your best" where Badge says "131.4K" (different sample figures). Welcome's "Just dropped" feed under the form is demo data; a real first run would show nothing there. Pop "Thick hide" row dimmed in the rare/paused sample is the fade-in, not a contrast bug.

## Broader UX
- Glanceable: Badge is the right default (name, +pickup, hour, ring). Buddy and Pop repeat the hour/session/best-here and should lose the zone name, the session total or the ring caption. Cut, don't restyle.
- States: only Pop and Buddy distinguish rare, paused and notice by shape (icon, frame, triangle). Badge, the strip and the classic warming use text colour only. Give Badge the same cue (a 14 px icon or a coloured edge), and make stopped read as an ending, not a sentence.
- Old style still in: ui-hud-warming-classic, the all-figures strip, the flag pill, and the "drag me" label; they use older type sizes and muted palette against the Quest pills. File names card/ticker now mean Buddy/Pop, which confuses the sweep.
- Options: In game is the longest page (shortcuts, panel, three looks drawn at real size, opacity, size, position, watch list, chime). Move the shortcuts to one page, collapse the watch list and chime under "Rare drops", and show only the chosen look's preview. Merge the tray toggles into one choice.
- Welcome: three clicks to Continue is good, but add one closing line or a "Start your first run" step, an input hint for gear score, and don't pre-tick sharing.
