# Image sources for dungeon, zone and boss artwork

Where a fan site can legitimately get Aion 2 imagery, what each source's reuse terms appear to be, and what the fan databases do. Checked 19-20 October 2026 with single page reads; nothing was crawled. "Verified" means the page was read directly; "unverified" means it came from search results or second-hand reporting and should be confirmed before relying on it.

Short version: no official source grants a general licence to reuse Aion 2 artwork. NC's terms claim ownership of all game content and restrict copying, scraping and commercial use, and say nothing about fan sites. The cleanly permitted route for official moving imagery is embedding the official YouTube videos. For stills, the realistic options are asking NC America's PR agency for press assets, or the owner's own screenshots with a clear unofficial-site disclaimer, which is common practice but is a judgement call, not a right. Until one of those exists the site should show marked placeholders rather than borrowed images.

## What this means for Kinah Ledger

1. Video: embed the official YouTube videos where a dungeon or zone needs moving imagery. This is the one route that is clearly permitted.
2. Stills: write to the PR agency named above and ask for press assets or permission for a non-commercial fan site. Keep the reply with the repository.
3. Own screenshots: the usual fan-site practice, with an unofficial-site disclaimer in the footer in the style aion2hub uses. NC's terms neither permit nor single this out; it rests on tolerance and fair-use style arguments, so it is the owner's decision, and it argues for keeping the site non-commercial (no ads, no paid tiers).
4. Do not copy from Steam, the fan databases, or social posts. Each is unlicensed, and copying from a fan site stacks a second unknown on top of the first.
5. Until one of 1 to 3 is in place, every dungeon, zone and boss slot in the UI shows a clearly marked placeholder, as the build already does for item data that has not been crawled.
6. Item icons are a separate, already-decided question: the site hot-links NC's CDN by default and `tools/fetch-icons.js` exists for a local mirror only if the owner chooses it. See `docs/data-pipeline.md`.
