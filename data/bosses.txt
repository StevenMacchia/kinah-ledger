# Bosses of the instances the tracker's Parser keeps fights for (Parser/BossBook.cs): "Instance|Kind|Boss|final", in route
# order, "final" on the instance's last boss (killing it clears the instance), "wave" on a wave stage (Ferocious Horn Den's
# Totem room: six Mau Totem Shamans and their adds, no single boss): a split of its own in Full run, never a boss pull. Kinds: Expedition, Sanctuary (the game's
# 10-player raids), Nightmare (the Nightmare instances; their loot stays uncounted, data/instances.txt "Ignored", but their
# boss pulls are kept), Ascension Trial (the weekly solo trial: Nightmare Altar one week, Sanctum of Loathing the next). A Transcendence is an expedition at a stage (Stage 1 to 4) with the same bosses, so it isn't listed apart:
# the game's map code marks one with G where the mode word stands (Arkanis_G_09_BossRoom_MapEvent, DeusCenter_G_09_MapEvent),
# and the Parser files it as a Transcendence at its stage. Season 1's are Shattered Arkanis and Deus Research Base
# (https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/transcendence, 2026-11-17: Exploration, and Transcendence Stage 1 to 4).
# World bosses are not listed here: the Parser takes their names and places from the boss timers (Parser/WorldBosses.cs).
# An instance listed here keeps fights with its listed bosses only (trash, elites and adds never make one); an instance not
# listed keeps fights with what the game itself marks a boss. A line "Instance|Kind" with no boss only files the instance.
# Source: the per-dungeon pages of https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/dungeons (2026-11-13); Snowfield of Sorrow from Inven Global

# Surveillance Device, Hydraulic Press); final flags for Deus Research Base and Fire Temple are from route order.
# Nightmare: https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/dungeons/nightmare (2026-11-17) lists no bosses, so Nightmare is filed with none and
# its pulls keep the game's names.

# (2026-10-23: Featherstorm Duduri and Watchful Raptor Dodori, fought together at the end). "with" marks a boss fought together
