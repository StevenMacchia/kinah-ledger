Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.63: your Market prices get shared again.

- **Your Market prices get shared again.** When the tracker misread a Market row as one long item name, the site turned down the whole batch, and the tracker kept sending that same batch every few seconds. Nothing you read on the Market after that reached your server's price board. Names the site can't take now stay on this PC, and a batch the site turns down is dropped instead of being sent over and over.
- **Waits when the site asks.** If the site says it is busy, the tracker waits as long as it asks before sending prices again.
- **Market rows read as rows.** On an item's listings page, the tracker sometimes saved a whole page of listings as one item's name, with prices and time left in it. Each listing now stays its own row, and a name made of numbers and times is never saved as an item.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
