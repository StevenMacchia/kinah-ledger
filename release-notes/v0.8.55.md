Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.55: the window shows what you're doing.

- **Pages switch on their own.** The tracker now opens on Farming every time. While you play, it turns to Crafting when you open the crafting window, to Events when you go into an expedition or an event, and back to Farming when you're out farming again. It waits a few seconds before switching, so a quick look at something else doesn't move it.
- **Your click wins.** If you pick a page yourself, it stays there until you start doing something else.
- **Prefer to switch by hand?** Turn off "Switch pages to what I'm doing" in Options › General.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
