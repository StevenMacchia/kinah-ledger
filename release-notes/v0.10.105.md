Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.105: a compact bag and a shorter warm-up.

- **A compact bag.** The open bag shows each item as a slim row: the icon, the name, the count and its worth, half the height it took. Three rows show at once, so most bags fit without "more". Hover an item for its full name and card.
- **Warming up is short.** Your hour shows after 30 seconds of farming and 6 things counted (or 2 minutes and 3), where it used to wait 2 full minutes. A gathering run showed "warming up" for 18 pickups before.
- **Full view stays level.** Drop odds moves between the two columns with Just dropped and Where to farm, so the columns end together.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
