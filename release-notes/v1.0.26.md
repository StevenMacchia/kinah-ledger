Kinah Ledger 1.0.26: your best drop and last three pickups show on every overlay look, all the time.

- **The pickup icons stay put.** Your best drop (with its star) and your last three pickups used to disappear from most looks whenever a fight showed beside the Kinah figures, a timer took the room, the figures grew long, or a dungeon run's words filled the line. They now stay on every look, farming, in a run, with timers due and during and after a fight. Where there isn't room beside the figures they move to a row of their own underneath instead of going away. The best drop shows once when it is one of the three.
- **Badge and Buddy show them too.** Badge now has the icons at the end of its line, and Buddy shows your best drop with its star in front of its three tiles.
- **Pop keeps three pickups under a notice.** A notice such as "Paused" now sits above Pop's pickups instead of taking one of their places.
- The icons still follow Settings › Buddy › More › Pickup icons, and nothing changes in the Abyss or with the meter in a dungeon.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
