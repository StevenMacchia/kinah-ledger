Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.49: the in-game overlay moves when something happens.

- **The figures roll.** On the Ticker, the hour and the session roll up to their new figures when you pick something up, the hour turning green for a moment as it rises, and the panel's edge lights gold.
- **The stack flows.** When a new pickup comes in on top, the ones below glide down to their new places instead of jumping.
- **Pickups pop.** A new pickup's worth pops in, and its icon grows in; a Legend, Unique, Epic or Special one glows once in its colour.
- **Moments.** A new top drop gets a gold shine across it, and a rare drop's glow beats twice as it lands.

Each plays once when something changes, and nothing loops, so the overlay costs the game nothing while it sits still. With Windows's animations turned off, none of it plays.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
