Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.2: expedition and event rewards counted from the reward table.

- At the end of an expedition or event, the reward table ("Obtained Items") is read slot by slot: each item is named from its icon and its count is read, and they go into that run on the Events tab. Kinah in the table counts as the run's Kinah, checked against your Kinah counter.
- Currencies and reward items that weren't on the item list now count: Kinah, Enhance Stone, Phantasmal Fragment, Abyss Points, Soul Codex, Silentium, Odyle Energy, and the shop and festival coins.
- Inside an expedition or event, loot belongs to the run only. It no longer shows as drops from kills, in Kinah by enemy, or in the site's farming numbers.
- A reward that arrives in the chat all at once after it faded is counted, and a level-up popup during an event no longer sets the run's loot aside.
- Every Shugo Festival game counts as "Shugo Festival" and every Dimensional Invasion as "Dimensional Invasion". An expedition's areas count as one run under the expedition's name.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
