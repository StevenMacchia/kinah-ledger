Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.46: the server pickers list servers A to Z, and Sealed Dungeons and Forts no longer count as expeditions.

- **Servers A to Z.** The first-run picker and Options › Server listed each region's servers in the game's pair order, which looked messy with the ten pairs added in 0.8.45 (2026-10-26). They now list Elyos servers A to Z, then Asmodian A to Z. Your saved server is unchanged.
- **Sealed Dungeons and Forts count as nothing.** They are open-world quest stops, but entering one was filed as an expedition run with 0 Kinah (nine of them on 2026-10-26). They no longer open a run or count as farming, and runs already saved in them are hidden from the Events tab, its averages and what is shared.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
