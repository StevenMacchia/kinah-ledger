Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.38: a won upgrade roll makes the upgrade.

- **Crafting upgrades.** When the 25% upgrade roll after a craft wins, the craft is now credited with the upgrade item (Artisan's Splendent Asvata Bow from Artisan's Asvata Bow), not the base item; the roll was counted before, but the output never changed. The loot list beside the character naming the upgrade right after a craft says the same, for a roll the panel reader missed. The upgrade landing in the loot list is the craft's output, not a drop.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
