Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.90: the Parser counts open-world fights live and never keeps them.

- **Open-world and Abyss fights are counted live, never kept.** In Every fight they show on the Parser tab while they last, then they're gone: no record, nothing saved on your PC, nothing sent. Fights in Transcendence, Expeditions and Sanctuaries are kept as before. Open-world fights that earlier versions kept are removed.
- **Your DPS while farming, small.** Fighting in the open world or the Abyss shows your DPS as one small figure on the Kinah overlay's line, in every look, and the overlay never grows for it. The big Parser section opens only inside Expeditions and other instanced runs. Settings › In game › Parser › Open-world DPS on the Kinah line turns it off.

- **Hidden Lugi has its name.** The Shugo Festival's Hidden Lugi now shows by name in your timers and runs, and an event's stage no longer steps back to "Getting ready" once it is under way.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
