Kinah Ledger 1.0.13: a speedrun timer in every look, a much lighter tracker, and Share fixed to the meter.

- **Run mode in every overlay look.** In a dungeon, the Kinah overlay turns into a run timer: the clock against your fastest clear here (green ahead, red behind), each boss's split against your best, and "Run 3 today · 38 clears". After the final boss, NEW BEST or "Cleared in 4:17 · 2nd fastest", with the run's worth against your average. Between runs, what you made here today. All from your own runs.
- **Much lighter on your PC.** The tracker no longer copies every frame of the game; it takes one only when it reads the screen. That frees most of a CPU core in dungeons, and the meter shows hits within a few hundredths of a second.
- **Share sits on the meter.** On the Kinah overlay, Share is now a tab on the meter's top-right edge. It no longer moves the overlay and is easy to click, click-through on or off.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
