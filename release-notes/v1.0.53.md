Kinah Ledger 1.0.53: the companion is now a tiny Shugo merchant

- **A Shugo in place of the winged wisp.** The optional companion is now a little white stoat merchant: round pink-lined ears, big black eyes, a turban with a jewel, a vest, a sash with a gold buckle and gold bangles. Turn it on from the Overlays button or Settings › Companion.
- **Dressed in your look.** Its clothes take the colours of the look your Buddy and meter use (gold for Classic, violet for Prism, red for Magma, crimson for Dragon, and so on for every look). The fur stays white.
- **Every reaction, acted out.** It flips a Kinah coin on a rare drop, wears a crown for a new best, hops and waves when a boss goes down, squints with its turban jewel flaring on a crit streak or a PvP kill, perks up its ears before a timer, sulks under a rain cloud after a wipe and topples over with its turban askew when you fall.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
