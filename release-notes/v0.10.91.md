Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.91: a new overlay, and your farming DPS rolls live.

- **A live DPS while you farm.** The small DPS on the Kinah overlay's line, and the Parser tab's "Your DPS now", follow your hits as they land. Each hit lifts the figure at once, and it slides down between hits, worked out ten times a second over your last few seconds of damage. When you stop, it eases onto the fight's figure instead of dropping to nothing or freezing. The digits keep their width, so the line doesn't twitch as the figure rolls.

- **A new overlay look, Auto, as the default.** A slim glass pill while you farm: your hour, the session and your live DPS, with a timer chip when something you watch is near. In a boss fight the group's meter opens above it, the boss and its HP on top and each member in their class colour. If you never chose a look, you move to Auto; a look you picked stays.
- **Three more looks to pick from.** Settings › In game › Panel look adds Capsule (the pill always), Meter (a raid meter always) and Float (no panel, just the figures over the game), each shown at its real size. Badge, Buddy and Pop are still there.
- **The separate timer strip and Parser share the new glass.**
- **Drag the overlay straight away.** On a new install the overlay is dragged from the start; letting clicks pass through to the game is a choice in Options.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
