Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.32: prices come from your own server, as it is now.

- **Only your server sets a price.** When your server's Market has no listing for an item, the tracker no longer borrows the other servers' middle price. The item shows as having no price on your server, and a craft that needs it asks you to type what you'd pay or tick "I have these". The other servers' price is still on the item card, for reference. (Siel had no Lesser Manastone listed, and the other servers' 80,000 each made an Intermediate Manastone craft look like it cost 400K.)
- **Yesterday's price doesn't count.** A board price last seen over a day ago is unsure and left out of totals and profit, the same as a single listing. It was three days before.
- **A thin market is flagged on the Crafting card.** When a material is priced on fewer than 10 listings and that material alone costs more than the craft makes, the card says so under the material ("only 6 listings, costs more than the craft makes") and the profit reads "unsure". The price stays as your server lists it. On the HUD, that profit has a "~" in front. A price you typed or read yourself, and a material you ticked "I have these", are never flagged.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
