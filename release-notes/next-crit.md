Kinah Ledger tracker: crit in the Parser.

- **Crit rate for every member and every skill.** A member's breakdown shows their crit rate, how much of their damage came from crits, and their biggest crit. Each ability has its own Crit column beside hits, max and min. The expanded overlay shows each member's crit rate too.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
