Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.1: counting keeps going with a window over the game.

- With the game read from its own window (new in 0.8.0), another window over the game, such as a browser or a chat app, no longer pauses counting. If the game is minimized, counting pauses as before.
- The panel over the game no longer blinks when the zone is read.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
