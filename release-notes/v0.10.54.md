Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.54: an expedition's reward table counts what is in it, and not the floor under it.

- **No more loot from the floor.** The reward table at the end of an expedition is now counted slot by slot from its top left, and it ends where the slots end. Before, a patch of floor a row or two below the table could look enough like a slot to stretch the table: a table of 27 was counted as 34 or 39, with gear you never got (a gold bow among it) in the run, the window and Buddy. This hit most Auldor's Sanctum tables and the Urugugu ones.
- **Tables over dark ground count in full.** In Fire Temple, Mirror Altar and Bakarmas Subspace the grey slots barely stand out from the ground, and many tables were counted short or not at all. They are now counted to their last slot.
- **A table still sliding in is found where it is.** The table can show up a little above or below where its "Reward collected." line puts it; the tracker now finds its rows within about 20 px either way. When it can't be sure, it counts less, never more.

Checked against 208 reward tables from the last week, counted by hand: 206 now count exactly right and none count too much (before: 121 right, 29 too much).

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
