Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.107: a live DPS meter that moves with your fighting.

- **Live DPS.** The meter redraws 20 times a second. It climbs as your hits land and falls every moment you don't hit, instead of changing once a second or holding still.
- **Real damage per second.** DPS is your damage over the time since the fight started, so stopping lowers it at once. (0.10.106 held it while you walked away; that's undone.)
- **Open world: a fight a pack.** With Always on, a few seconds without fighting ends the fight. Its result stays on the meter, and your next hit starts from 0. Each boss in a run still starts from 0.
- **Runs keep their own hour.** While a run you started is under way, the all-zones Kinah per hour holds: none of the run's loot or time goes into it, and it goes on when the run stops. Today still counts everything.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
