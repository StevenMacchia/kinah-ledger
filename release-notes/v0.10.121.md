Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.121: the meter's big moments, directed.

- **The end of a fight plays as a sequence.** A blade of light cuts across the meter, a kill gives it a small punch, and a shine runs down the bars from the top. Then the TOP DPS and TOP HEALS plates drop in and glint. Each step waits for the one before, so nothing lands on top of anything else.
- **A personal best gets its own moment, last.** The PB chip drops in with a flash, a ring of gold sparks bursts off it, your DPS flares gold and settles back, and your frame pulses.
- **Your row catches fire.** When your pace beats your best on a boss, a wave of flame runs across your row. It then keeps burning: flames flicker along its foot behind the text, embers rise off it, and your rank chip glows like a coal. Your name and DPS stay readable.
- **Light on the meter.** The fire is a handful of small shapes updated with the meter's own redraws. Nothing runs when the meter is hidden, and nothing moves if Windows animations are off.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
