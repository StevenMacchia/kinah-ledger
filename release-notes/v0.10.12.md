Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.12: the window stays on top when you want it to, and the pages have keys.

- **Keep above other windows.** A switch in Options › General and in the Farming page's ⋯ menu keeps the window over the game and everything else, for a windowed game on one screen. Off by default; the panel over the game is on top either way.
- **Ctrl+1, 2, 3** show Farming, Crafting and Events, as the tabs sit. The keys are listed on Shortcuts & tips.
- **Two more files for a spreadsheet.** The Crafting page's Save as CSV, beside Start over, writes This session's recipes as the table prices them: tries, worked and failed, what came out, the cost (and whether every material was priced), what it sells for after the Market's cut, the profit, the upgrade rolls. The Farming page's ⋯ menu gains Save this week as CSV…, a row a day for seven days: what farming made, the Kinah, time farming, kills, gathering attempts and zones, with the day's runs, how many counted, their Kinah and the places.
- **Two header buttons have names for a screen reader**: the site button and the Compact / Full view button.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
