Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.12: a Market buy is not a drop.

- **Bought at the Market.** An item bought from the Market prints only its own "acquired" line, and the Market's window covers the chat while it is open, so the line was read later and counted as a drop out in the open world (Wrathful Mind, 2026-11-06). Now anything that lands while the Market is open, or in the first read of the chat after it was open, is a buy: it goes to the reward lines skipped, never to the drops or the loot per hour. That holds for a rare too, which otherwise counts with no kill beside it.
- **The mail's attachments.** "Obtained items attached to mail." is the inventory at work, like storage and Quick Register: what prints with it, and in the ten seconds after, was claimed, not dropped.
- **Replay.** The trace records when the Market was open, and a replay of an older trace takes it from reads.log, so a report of this kind replays as it will count from now on.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
