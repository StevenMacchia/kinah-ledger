Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.1: event rewards, the Kinah check and runs closing on time.

- **Event cards count only the cards you got.** On a five-card reward screen (Shugo Festival and others), a read taken while the cards were still flipping could take a card you didn't get as received and add its items to the run. Which cards you got is now decided by the screen once it has settled, and a re-read from the run's pictures wins when it disagrees.
- **Kina pickups aren't refused over a misread balance.** The tracker checks Kina against your Kinah balance on screen. When it misread the balance by a million (a "1," lost or added), it took that as spending, and could refuse a real pickup. A balance that jumps like that now has to be read the same way for 20 seconds before it counts.
- **Runs close on time.** After you leave an expedition or event, the run now closes about a minute later on its own. Before, it could stay open until your next pickup or zone change, sometimes for half an hour.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings, goals and history are kept.
