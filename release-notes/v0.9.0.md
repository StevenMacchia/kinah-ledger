Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.0: set goals for the day, and look back over your week.

- **Goals.** Set up to three goals for today on the Farming page: Kinah from farming, an item (from drops, gathering or run rewards), or a number of runs (expeditions, events or any). Each goal is a ring around its tile with the count under it; hover it to see what you have, what's left and when you'll get there at today's pace. Set one from the ⋯ menu, an item's card, a right-click on a LOOT slot, or the + slot. When you reach a goal its ring turns gold and the tracker says so once. No sound.
- **Your week.** LOOT has a new Week side: what you made, your time and your rate over the last seven days, with a bar per day. Pick a day to see its zones. The Events strip gets Today and Week too, with each day's runs and their Kinah.
- History starts from the day you install 0.9 (today's farming is carried over), and keeps the last 60 days on your PC.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
