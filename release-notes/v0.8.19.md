Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.19: what your expeditions made, per run and in total.

- Expeditions and events now show a TOTAL column beside PER RUN: everything made in that place over the runs shown (Kinah plus loot you can sell, at the lowest Market price after the Market's cut; bound gear isn't counted).
- The top line says what all your runs made together.
- Click a place to see each run: when it started, how long it took and what it made, above its drops. A run whose reward screen wasn't read says so instead of guessing.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
