Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.119: the Parser's width is yours.

- **Buddy's width by default.** The Parser's own window is now as wide as the Kinah overlay's Buddy panel. If you never changed the width, you get the new one.
- **Drag its edges.** Drag the left or right edge of the Parser's window to make it wider or narrower. It's kept the moment you let go. Dragging the left edge keeps the right edge where it was.
- **It stays put.** Opening or closing Settings no longer resets the width, and an expanded meter keeps its extra room. The width only changes when you change it.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
