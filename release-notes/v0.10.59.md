Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.59: bound materials cost nothing in a craft.

- **No more fake losses on bound crafts.** A material you've picked up bound copies of (Lesser Manastone (Bound) from a dungeon, say) can't be sold, so it costs nothing in a craft. The Crafting part used to charge it at the Market's price, which made every Manastone craft look like a big loss.
- **What a bound craft makes is bound too.** It can't be sold either, so it isn't valued at the Market's price. A bound craft says "bound" instead of a profit, and its cost is only what you paid for: the NPC's ink, and any Market material you have no bound copies of.
- **On the craft card**, a material held bound reads "bound · 0", and the card says it's a bound craft instead of a profit per craft. Untick "I have these" on a material if you're using ones you bought.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
