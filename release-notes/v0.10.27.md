Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.27: the Quests tab, three bars on Next up, smaller points.

- **Next up is three bars.** The round Kinah figure this session is about to pass, then the two crafting materials you last farmed (a Radiant or Fine variant takes the row over the plain one), and nothing else. A quest you reach shows on the panel over the game with its points for a few seconds and leaves the list. A click on a bar still opens its tree.
- **The Quests tab.** Everything else moved there: your score this session, today, this week and all time; every active quest with its tree and a pin (a pinned quest takes one of Next up's material rows); today's repeatable quests with how many days running; all-time chains with the big targets, from a tally this PC keeps; your own goals, where Set a goal lives now; and what you have done, by day.
- **Smaller points, Kinah first.** A session is tens of points, a heavy week about a thousand: Kinah steps are worth twice a run's and three times a material's, rarer materials more, today's and the week's quests more than the session's, sixty points an hour at most. The points earned under the old scale are reset; the history of what you reached is kept.
- **Quests on or off.** Options › General › Quests, on by default. Off takes the tab, the rows, the points and the upload away.
- **To your profile.** With sharing on, completed quests go up with each upload, so your profile on kinahledger.com can show them and the leaderboards can rank the score. The site's side arrives in its own update.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
