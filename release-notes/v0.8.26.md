Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.26: gathering counts, late pickups count, and crafting knows what you farmed.

- Gathering is counted from the gathering bar. The bar sits higher on screen than the crafting panel and the tracker was looking in the wrong place, so a gather with a busy chat was often lost. Each gather now counts on its own Success, chat or no chat, and two gathers of the same herb in a row are two.
- A pickup whose chat line arrives late is counted once, not dropped. This also fixes Kinah pickups that went missing when chat was busy.
- For a minute after an event or expedition reward screen, the rewards in chat are rewards, never farming or gathering.
- Crafting: tick "I have these" on a material you farmed, gathered or already own and it costs nothing; the recipe card then says what selling those materials instead would bring, and which way wins.
- A material with no price no longer blanks a recipe's cost and profit: you see "at least" and "up to" with the known part, and the totals say how many materials are unpriced.
- NPC shop prices for every vendor-sold crafting material come with the tracker. Type your own in the slot if one is off.
- Item names are matched against what the loot list could show in that place, and garbled reads outside that table are refused rather than guessed.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
