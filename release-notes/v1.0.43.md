Kinah Ledger 1.0.43: a new theme, Atreia, and an arcade cabinet for the meter.

- **Atreia, a new theme.** Four looks drawn from the game's world, for the Kinah overlay and the DPS meter. Pick them in Settings › Buddy or DPS meter › Theme.
- **Rift.** The Abyss torn open: violet glass full of stars, a jagged wound of light across every panel, and a crystal crown on the leader. Every pull rips the glass open.
- **Elyos.** Navy and gold leaf under a winged sun. Great ivory wings spread from the leader's row, and a beam of light opens the meter on every pull.
- **Asmodian.** Black-iron war gear with horns, a thorn crest and a blood moon. A horned crown slams onto whoever takes the lead, with embers.
- **Prism.** Cut crystal: a faceted shard frame, and every bar splits the light into a rainbow beam. The crown prism turns white light into the leader's spectrum.
- **Arcade, new in Wacky.** A real cabinet: a lit marquee, LED score digits, 1ST to 5TH in name-entry colours and a pixel crown. READY? FIGHT! opens each pull, and STAGE CLEAR fireworks mark a kill.
- **Moments, not noise.** Each look plays its effects only when something happens (a pull, a new leader, a kill, your best passed, a rare drop) and sits still while you read it. The figures never move, and nothing covers them.
- **Everywhere.** Every new look works in both windows, at any width and size, in Korean and Chinese, and with the PB ghost.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
