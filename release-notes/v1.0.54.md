Kinah Ledger 1.0.54: honest hourly figures again, full-length Prism bars and a meter you can resize

- **No more made-up millions.** A single joke listing on the market (one pair of common gloves listed at 10,000,999 Kinah) could count as that item's price and blow up your Kinah per hour. A lone listing far above what the item sells for on the other servers no longer counts as its price. Today's figures correct themselves after the update.
- **Prism bars run the whole row.** In the Prism look the top player's bar stopped near the middle, more so with the meter expanded. The bars now run the full width with the figures on top, like the other looks.
- **No more "+0/s".** The healing figure beside a name only shows when there is real healing.
- **Resize and expand the meter again.** In looks with a glow around them (like Prism), the edges you drag to resize sat off to the side of the meter. They are back on the meter's own edges. With click-through on, a click on the meter's handle now expands or folds it.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
