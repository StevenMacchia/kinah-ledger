Kinah Ledger tracker 0.7.6. Trackers on 0.7.2 and later update by themselves; otherwise download KinahLedger.exe and run it.

- The panel over the game shows what you pick in Options › In game: timer, total per hour, Kinah per hour, loot per hour, session total, your best here, last pickup. Every figure is the window's own, so the two always agree.
- Your best hour in each zone, per server: the run card says "Your best here", or "Best here yet" when this stay beats it. A stay counts once it has ten minutes in the zone.
- Copy for Discord: one click puts a picture of the count (zone, per hour, top drops) on the clipboard for Ctrl+V. A copy is saved in the cards folder.
- Summary first: the session total, how it adds up and the three best drops sit right under the run card. Empty tables are hidden, and each one appears and opens when its first loot lands.
- The window is never taller than your screen: the run card and the summary stay put, and the pickups and tables scroll below them. Tables show their top three rows (Show all for the rest), and loot from other zones is one line instead of a header per table.
- Game notices at the top of the screen ("Title acquired", "Quest completed") are no longer mistaken for the enemy on the target bar, and the "not on the enemy list" note clears after 30 seconds.
- No more Open world / Gathering picker: which one you're doing is read from the chat. The button is now Start run.
- Plain rows: how a pickup or drop was read ("recovered", "guessed", "loot list", "not on any table") moved into the hover text. A ⓘ marks rows that have more to say.
- Prices: an item nobody has listed on your server yet takes the middle price of the other servers in your region and faction, marked ≈.
- New installs keep counting in the tray when the window is closed or minimized. Existing settings are kept.
- Crafting's cost column reads COST, so it fits.

Settings, history and logs are kept.
