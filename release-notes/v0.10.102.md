Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.102: pick how the Parser's own window looks.

- **Parser looks are back in Settings.** Settings › In game › Parser › Overlay look offers Standard, Auto, Capsule, Meter and Float for the Parser's own window, with a large preview of each. Hover a look to see it; click to pick it.
- **Apart from the Kinah overlay.** The look you pick is the Parser window's own: the Kinah overlay keeps its look.
- **The Parser page** names the look in use, one click from changing it.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
