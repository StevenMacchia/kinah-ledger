Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.10: world boss loot on the site.

- With sharing on, what you pick up when a world boss goes down (Saursus, Bloodfang, Pnyn, Kutar) is shared as that boss's loot. The site shows each boss's loot table, rarest first, in its own World bosses section on the Zones page. It never counts toward Kinah per hour, farming averages or events.
- Bound Kinah from an expedition's or Transcendence's end reward now counts toward that run's Kinah.
- Opening an Odyle Energy Cube is no longer taken for crafting, so what it gives stays loot.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
