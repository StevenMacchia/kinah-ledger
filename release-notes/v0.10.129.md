Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.129: the time left to enter, and timers as badges.

- **3 minutes to enter.** The Shugo Festival and the Dimensional Invasion only let you in for the first 3 minutes after they start. Their timers now keep counting through those minutes: "Shugo ENTER 2:41" in green on the overlay, and "2m 41s left to enter" in the Timers window. When the entry closes, they move on to the next start.
- **Timers as badges.** On the overlay, each timer is now its own small badge: green while you can enter, gold in its last minutes, grey otherwise. They replace the line of yellow text on a black box, on every overlay look and on the separate timer strip.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
