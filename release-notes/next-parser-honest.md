Kinah Ledger tracker: the Parser counts honestly and says what it counts.

- **No "Other" row, nothing left out unsaid.** Damage nobody's hit can be credited to is no longer a hidden row. The group card now says how much of the boss's HP is credited to your group, like "99.8% of the boss's HP credited", and hovering it shows how much wasn't. Group DPS and shares count the credited damage, and the line says so.
- **Boss damage and adds apart.** On a boss pull, everyone's DPS, share and bar count damage to the boss. A member's breakdown shows their damage to adds beside it. When a fight has adds, a Boss / All targets switch sits on the group card.
- **Fight time or active time, one click away.** The group card has a Fight / Active switch, so you no longer need Settings to change what DPS is over. A member's breakdown also shows their active time and how much of the fight they spent idle.
- **Pulls timed from the first hit to the last.** A pull now starts at the first damage, not seconds before it. A wipe ends at the group's last damage, even when the boss just resets or the end is never announced; such a pull used to run on for up to 10 minutes. If you leave mid-pull, it ends at your last hit.
- **Late joiners and leavers marked.** Someone who joins mid-fight is tagged "joined late", and someone who leaves is tagged "left", even after the damage has started. Hover the tag to see when it happened.
- **No inflated DPS in short fights.** Under 3 seconds DPS shows "–" rather than a big number from a single hit.
- **Honest hit counts.** When a hit count is missing, Hits and Average show "–" instead of a made-up 1, and the average a hit only uses hits that were counted.
- **Your deaths counted once.** Each of your own deaths could count twice; now each counts once.
- **Deaths on by default.** The group's deaths, the list filters and the explanations on hover are now on for everyone. If you had turned any Parser extras on yourself, your choices stay. "Kills and deaths" in Settings › In game › Parser is now two switches: Deaths, and Kills near you.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
