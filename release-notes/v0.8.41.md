Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.41: the Ticker look stops erroring, the Card and Ticker show your crafting, and long recipe names are counted as their recipe.

- **The Ticker look no longer throws "Specified Visual is already a child".** Two pickups read in the same instant with the same name and amount made the Ticker add one toast twice (2026-10-26, 10:05). It now keeps one toast per pickup. Counting was never affected.
- **Crafting shows on the Card and the Ticker.** Until now those two looks only showed farming and event pickups, so a crafting session looked like nothing was recorded (2026-10-26). While the crafting window is open the panel switches to crafting by itself: the Card shows the recipe, time spent, crafts, failed, profit and "sells for", with the last three crafts as tiles marked ✓ or ✕; the Ticker shows the profit and counts in its pill, and each craft slides in as a toast. Every figure matches the window's Crafting tab. The Strip keeps its own crafting line.
- **Long recipe names count as their recipe.** The game's crafting panel cuts long names ("Splendent Orichalcum Longsword (Novic..."), and each cut name was filed as its own recipe with no icon, no price and 0 tries (2026-10-26, 10:12). The cut name is now matched to the recipe being crafted, so the tries, the upgrade rolls and the Splendent you won are credited to Orichalcum Longsword.
- **Upgrades sit under their recipe in the Crafting tab.** Each upgrade you made is an indented row under the base recipe, with how many were made, its icon, what it sells for and how much more it is worth than the items it replaced. The recipe's failed count is in its tooltip.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
