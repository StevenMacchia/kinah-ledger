Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.47: a reward card you pick more than once now counts once per pick, and two kinds of farming drops are no longer missed.

- **Cards picked more than once.** On Shugo Festival and Dimensional Invasion reward screens you can pick the same card up to four times, and each pick gives its whole list again. The tracker counted each card once (2026-10-27: a Beritra Air Raid card picked three times kept only a third of its gear). It now reads the number on each picked card and counts its list that many times. If it can't read the number, it counts the list once and marks the run as one to look at.
- **Drops after storage or the wardrobe.** Storing items or unlocking a skin sets aside what prints in the next 10 seconds, since those items moved rather than dropped. That window restarted on every read while the line was still on screen, so a real drop up to half a minute later could be lost. It now runs 10 seconds from when the line first appears.
- **Drops right after an event.** For a minute after an event's or expedition's reward screen, new pickups count as that screen's rewards. A drop that comes with a kill in that minute, and isn't on the screen's rewards, now counts as a drop.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
