Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.93: every gathered find, the moment you get it.

- **Gathering counts at once.** Everything a gather gives, the ore and any rare find like Poko's Skin Bundle, counts the moment you get it. A Fine or Pure ore that was the first of its kind this session used to show up as much as 15 seconds late, and a rare find picked up together with ore could be missed; both are fixed.
- **The Parser sits beside Craft and Runs.** The header reads Farm · Craft · Runs · Parser · Timers, and the Parser is always there. Ctrl+4 opens the Parser and Ctrl+5 the Timers.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
