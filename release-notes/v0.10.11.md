Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.11: the window at the size you want.

- **Window size.** Options › General › Window size draws the whole window bigger or smaller, 80% to 150%, for a large or high-resolution screen, or to keep it out of the way. It shows as the slider moves and is kept on Save. In the window, Ctrl+= is a step bigger, Ctrl+- a step smaller and Ctrl+0 is back to 100%; the status line says the size. The screen still caps it: the window never grows past the width or the height of the screen it is on, and Full view keeps shrinking to fit a narrow one as before.
- **Keyboard focus in the window's own look.** Moving through the window and Options with Tab used to draw Windows's dotted rectangle on the button or switch that had focus. It is now a thin gold ring, and still shows only when focus moves by keyboard, never on a click.
- **Save as CSV.** The Farming page's ⋯ menu saves today's loot as a CSV file for a spreadsheet: the Kinah, every drop and gathered item, how many, how many bound, what they are worth after the Market's cut and the zones each came from. The ALL RUNS box on the Expeditions and events page saves the runs it shows the same way, each with when it started, the place, how long it took, its Kinah, kills, worth, items and anything to look at. A dialog asks where; nothing is uploaded.
- **Ctrl+wheel** over the window steps its size too.
- **Sliders in the window's look.** The size, opacity and volume sliders in Options were Windows's own; they now match the rest.
- **The first-run screen is only the first-run screen.** The Farming, Crafting and Events tabs and the site button wait with Options until a server is chosen; there was nothing to switch to before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
