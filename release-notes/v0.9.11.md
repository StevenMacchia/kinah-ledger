Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.11: the zones in Where to farm now do something when you click them.

- **A zone row is a button.** Hover a row in Where to farm now and it lights up. With Compare zones and runs on (Options › General), a click turns LOOT to Week › Zones with that zone picked beside the one you are in. If you haven't farmed it 10 minutes this week, the line at the foot says so. With Compare off, a click opens the zone's page on kinahledger.com, and the tooltip says where to turn Compare on.
- **Right-click for the zone's page.** A right-click on any row opens that zone's page on kinahledger.com, whether Compare is on or off.
- **The zone you are in stands out.** Its row has a light gold background and a gold bar at its left edge, in every look, as well as "you are here".
- **Your best hour beside theirs.** Where you have a best hour in a zone on your server (the "best here" under the hour), the row shows "your best" next to what players make there.
- **Each row's tooltip says what a click does.**
- **Where to farm now skips what can't be trusted.** A pooled 'unknown zone' row and any figure no one could really make no longer appear.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
