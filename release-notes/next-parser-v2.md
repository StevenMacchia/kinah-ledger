Kinah Ledger tracker: Parser v2, a meter that feels like part of the tracker.

- **A new Parser page.** The group's DPS is the big figure. Your own card sits beside it in gold, with your DPS, your place and how you're doing against your best kill of this boss and your last pull of it. The boss's HP is on a ring and a glowing bar.
- **The group at a glance.** Each member has a rank chip in their class colour, with DPS and share, and damage when Group details is on. Your row is framed in gold. A death puts a red mark on that member's row.
- **Inspect a member.** Click a member to see every ability with its icon, damage, share, DPS, hits and average, sortable by any column. It also shows their hits, average hit, active time and deaths.
- **Compare with your best.** On a boss you've killed before, the Compare tab sets this pull against your best kill and your last pull, overall and ability by ability. It's worked out from the fights kept on your PC, and other players' figures never leave it.
- **A bolder overlay in every look.** Rows sit on thick class-coloured bars with angled rank chips. Your row is framed in gold and stays in sight even when you're below the top four. Click the meter (or use Settings › In game › Parser › Expanded meter) to show everyone with their damage.
- **Motion that tells you what happened.** Figures count, bars slide, a member who overtakes another glides into their place and their rank chip flips. A kill sweeps a light across the meter. Beat your best on a boss and your row bursts gold with your new mark. A death's mark drops onto the member's row.
- **One app.** Entering a dungeon, the Kinah overlay unfolds into the meter, and it folds back when you leave. On the Runs page, a run now lists its boss fights. Click one and it opens in the Parser, and Back takes you to the run.
- **Only real figures.** Crit, max hit, casts, damage taken, the timeline and the death recap are built and will show once the tracker records them. Until then they stay hidden rather than guessed.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
