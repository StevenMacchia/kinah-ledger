Kinah Ledger 1.0: a free Windows tracker for Aion 2. It shows exactly what your play time earns, gives you a boss parser that measures your group, and keeps every world boss and event timer, while you play. 1.0 is the end of two months of weekly releases. Everything below is in it.

- **Exact Kinah and loot per hour.** Every Kinah, pickup and kill counts the moment it happens, at your server's Market prices after the 12% cut. Bound items are kept apart and given no value; gear you haven't equipped is priced, since gear binds on equip.
- **Crafts and gathering, counted.** Craft tries, combos and the profit at your server's prices. Every gathering try is counted, upgrades and fails included, with rare finds called out.
- **A boss parser built for min-maxing.** Group DPS live, Combat Power beside every name, each ability by name, healing (effective, with overheal on hover), boss HP with ⅓ marks, a death recap, and your pull against your best on that fight. Alone on the meter, the foot shows your best on the fight or your peak 5 seconds. Records are kept per boss, instance and mode. Nothing about your fights leaves your PC unless you turn on Show my boss bests.
- **World bosses and Nightmare, logged.** Pulls of the world bosses on the timer list, and the bosses of Nightmare, are kept in the parser's history under their names, with their HP bars, outcome and your bests, like any dungeon boss.
- **Themes.** The overlays come in Classic, Wacky, Tactical and Glitch, nine looks in all, each with its own skills card. New in 1.0: the app's window has matching Wacky, Tactical and Glitch skins, and picking an overlay theme offers to match the app.
- **The Abyss as one zone.** Abyss Points and Rank Points an hour, PvP and monster kills apart, and a share picture for your Abyss session.
- **Every world boss and event, on time.** Field bosses, Shugo Festival, Dimensional Invasion and more on your region's time, with the entry window counted down, a chime before the ones you watch, and a list that pops out to a second screen.
- **Overlays that stay with you.** A Kinah overlay and a separate meter overlay; drag them anywhere, resize them, click-through on or off. They stay up when you tab out, and go away when the game is minimized or closed.
- **Each character on its own.** Numbers, runs, gear, records and quests are kept per character, and every character's daily quests go up to your profile, not only the one you're playing.
- **Your profile on kinahledger.com.** Link the tracker and your runs, best hours, Abyss and daily quests are on your profile on any device.
- **English, Korean and Traditional Chinese** across the whole app.

Windows 10 or 11, no administrator rights, and it updates itself. Play in borderless or windowed mode. Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
