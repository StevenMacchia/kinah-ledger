Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.88: shortcuts never pop up a warning.

- **No more "already used by another app" pop-ups.** If a shortcut can't be used because another app has it, the tracker says so once, quietly, in the line under that shortcut in Settings › Shortcuts & tips. There's no tray notice, no line on the Kinah overlay and no status message. If it's another copy of Kinah Ledger holding it, nothing is said at all.
- **One tracker owns the shortcuts.** Only the copy of Kinah Ledger that's running as your tracker sets up shortcuts and a tray icon. Another copy started beside it leaves them alone.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
