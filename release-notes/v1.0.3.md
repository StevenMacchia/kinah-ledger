Kinah Ledger 1.0.3: the DPS meter works on trash in dungeons.

- **Live DPS on every pack.** Inside an expedition, a Transcendence or a Sanctuary, the DPS meter now shows your DPS on trash too, each pack from 0. It no longer waits for the first boss. Only boss fights are kept in the history, as before.
- **Ferocious Horn Den on Easy.** The new Easy mode is named and kept as Ferocious Horn Den (Easy), not "Easy".

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
