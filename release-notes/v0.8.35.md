Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.35: Giant Sparkie kept apart from Sparkie, and a Discord link.

- **Enemies with similar names.** The enemy table no longer folds one enemy into another with a similar name, such as Aullaeu Forest Giant Sparkie into Aullaeu Forest Sparkie. When two enemies of the area share a soul and neither is on your target bar, the kill shows under the soul's own name ("Sparkie") rather than going to the wrong one. The tracker still doesn't read a Giant Sparkie's target bar; that fix comes once we have a screenshot of it.
- **Discord.** A Discord link sits beside Report a miscount, on the About page and under Updates; post miscount zips in #bugs-and-feedback.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
