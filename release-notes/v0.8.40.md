Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.40: crafts, rewards and the Kinah counter counted right, and a panel that behaves like a game overlay.

- **A craft queue is counted as crafts.** Crafting 21 Wind Serums (2026-10-25, 20:25) showed 1 craft and 19 "unknown node" gathering attempts, because the panel's title read as nonsense and the panel vanished for a moment between crafts. The recipe line ("Wind Serum (Novice Lv. 10)") now proves you are crafting, crafting lasts through a short gap, and the same queue counts 15 crafts that worked, 4 that failed and no gathering. A garbled title is never taken for the recipe.
- **Reward pictures keep what they read.** An Amplify Stone Fragment x9 wrapped onto two chat lines (2026-10-25, 20:06) made the picture's slot "not read"; a chat count that was only guessed can no longer overrule a picture. Reward tables no longer read nameplates and health bars under the grid as extra slots (44 for 29 at 13:53), and two reads of an event screen that disagree on a digit leave that slot to look at instead of keeping the first (Abyss Points x340 for x320 at 18:06).
- **Loot list names.** A row reading "Accursed or" was named Accursed Bow from a doubtful icon (2026-10-25, 13:43); when the text clearly names an item, the text now wins. "Daeva Pass Shop Coin x2,OOO" reads as 2,000 (13:48).
- **Pickups count when they land.** A game window over the chat (the pet panel, a storage window) is no longer taken for a chat read, and the catch-up after unreadable reads closes within seconds, so a pickup is no longer dated up to 40 s early (157 Kina at 16:38 on 2026-10-25). Candy Key Fragment (Event) and Balaur's Essence are now known items.
- **The Kinah counter is read again.** From 0.8.26 to 0.8.39 the currency strip's Kinah figures were never read (seen in the 2026-10-25 logs), so Kina pickups went unchecked. The strip is now read one figure at a time and its first figure is no longer cut off, so Kina pickups are verified against the counter again; bound Kina and Kinah you spend no longer count as misses.
- **The panel behaves like a game overlay.** It shows while the game is in front and gets out of the way on alt-tab. New: lock it so clicks go through to the game (Move the panel is in the tray menu), and a panel size from 70 to 200%. The dot is red and the timer says RUN only during a run, and the shortcut confirms "Run started" / "Run stopped" without bringing the tracker over the game. Updates wait until you leave the game, and the first two minutes say "warming up" instead of a wild per-hour figure.
- **Two new panel looks.** Options › In game › Panel look offers Card (your hour, session and last three pickups as item tiles) and Ticker (a small pill with pickup toasts) beside the Strip, drawn at real size so you can compare them. A rare drop lights them in its grade colour.
- **A calmer tracker window.** Full view shrinks to fit a narrow screen, or shows Compact with a note. Clearing tables, Start over, removing or stopping a run happen at once with an Undo bar for 8 seconds instead of "Are you sure?". The start/stop shortcut can no longer be a key Windows or typing needs. Price alerts also show on the panel, since Windows holds back tray notices while a game is in front. Panel settings in Options show at once, Save keeps them and Cancel puts them back.
- **A lighter reading pass.** Looking for the crafting panel took about 116 ms of every pass on a 4K-wide screen; it now takes a few, so each pass is about a fifth shorter.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
