Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.52: a new recipe no longer leaves a stray row in Crafting, whatever you craft.

- **One queue, one recipe.** Every craft in a crafting queue is the same recipe. The tracker now settles which recipe it is once per queue. Before, each craft went under whatever the crafting window's title last read as, so a garbled first read of a new recipe became a row of its own. Now a queue's crafts wait until the title reads as a recipe the tracker knows (usually one craft later), or until the same name reads twice, then all go under it.
- **Crafts under a guess move.** If the title stays unreadable for 30 seconds, the crafts show under the best guess so the window keeps up. They move to the right recipe as soon as its name reads.
- **A quick switch still counts.** Starting another recipe within a few seconds of the last one is caught when its name reads twice in a row. A single read of another name is treated as a misread.
- Tested against every recipe in the recipe book: each queue opens on a garbled title read, and every craft lands on its own recipe.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
