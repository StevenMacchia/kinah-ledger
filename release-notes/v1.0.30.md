Kinah Ledger 1.0.30: much less memory, bars that read like a meter, and every meter look on offer.

- **Far less memory.** Reading the screen left each picture it read stuck in memory, so on a big screen the tracker could pass 1 GB within minutes. It now lets go of every picture as soon as it's read.
- **The top player's bar runs full.** Each other player's bar is their damage against the top player's, the way other meters draw them. The share of the group's damage is still the % beside the figure.
- **Every meter look, any time.** With the meter inside the Kinah overlay, the look page still offers every theme and every look. Picking one from another theme gives the meter its own overlay in that look, and the Kinah overlay keeps its own.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
