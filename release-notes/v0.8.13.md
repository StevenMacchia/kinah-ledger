Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.13: a check on every pickup, fewer missed kills, fewer double counts.

- Every pickup is checked before it counts. Out in the world, an item counts as a drop only when a kill or a gather happened within a few seconds of it. Claims, mail, reward cubes, storage, Quick Register and quest items no longer land in your drops.
- Skins unlocked in the wardrobe ("Dawn Frost (Gloves) Skin acquired") are not drops, and neither is a skin from a skin box.
- Stacks no enemy drops (Enhance Stone x446, Oath Coin x680) are rewards, not drops.
- Kina never shows as an item in Drops from kills. It counts only in Kinah per hour.
- Soul Crystals from a quest ("Protect the Odyle Collector completed") are not counted with Soul Crystals from kills, so the per hour stays right.
- Kills are counted more completely. A new Kina line that looked like the last one ("285 Kina" after "286 Kina") is no longer taken for it, and a kill quest's counter ("Defeat ... (21/25)") now helps count kills whose loot line was lost.
- Fewer lines are counted twice when the chat garbles and then settles.
- Event rewards are no longer lost when the chat fades right after showing them, and a reward line the chat wraps in two ("(Bound) x2") keeps its count.
- Closing the game, or logging in on another character, saves the run so far and starts counting fresh.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
