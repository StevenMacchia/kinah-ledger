Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.16: the tracker knows which event you're in, and an event's rewards stay with that event.

- The tracker keeps one clear "what you're doing now": farming in a zone, an event and the event family it belongs to, an expedition, a world boss, crafting, the Market, a reward screen, or idle.
- Events are recognised from three things at once: the minimap, the chat's "joined event" line and the event's own NPC lines, plus the event header under the minimap. Each run is named with its event and its family.
- An event's rewards that print just after you leave stay with that event, and a kill or loose Kina after it closes the run, so your farming doesn't get the event's loot and the event doesn't get your farming.
- Leaving an event and coming straight back starts a new run, and a run paused for a few minutes ends where it was.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
