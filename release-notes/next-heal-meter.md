- **A heal meter.** A healer's row (anyone who heals someone else: a Cleric, a Chanter) gets a thin green line along its foot, as long as their share of the group's healing. Under their name is what they healed, per second, and "≈… eff", the part that landed rather than overhealed (the ≈ says it's an estimate). On a narrow meter the "eff" part gives way first. Self-heals and potions count in a member's healing but never give them the line.
- **TOP HEALS.** At a boss's end, the group's top healer gets a green TOP HEALS plate beside the gold TOP DPS.
- **The last heal counts.** A heal that lands in a boss's last moment, just after the kill, now shows in the result the meter holds.
