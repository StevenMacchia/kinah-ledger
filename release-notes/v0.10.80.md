Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.80: a Parser for boss fights, whole runs or every fight.

- **A new Parser tab, in three modes.** The tab names what each mode counts:
  - **Bosses only** (the default): each boss pull in Transcendence, Expeditions and Sanctuaries. Trash and the open world never count, and every new boss starts fresh.
  - **Full run**: each run of those, from entering to leaving, trash included. Every boss is a split with its time and result, and a run is cleared when its final boss dies.
  - **Every fight**: fights anywhere, the open world and the Abyss included. Each lasts from the first hit or kill to 20 seconds without one, with your kills (players counted apart) and your deaths.
- **What's under way, live.** You see the boss or the run, a running clock, the boss's HP, and your group with each member's class, Combat Power and item level. Damage and DPS show only when the game provides them, and are never estimated.
- **Everything is kept on this PC, per character.** Past records can be filtered by mode, by kind (Transcendence, Expedition, Sanctuary, the open world, the Abyss) and by boss or instance, to see your record and best time. Open one for its group, its splits, deaths, timings and kills. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Robust by design.** A lost connection, a reconnect or a tracker restart doesn't split a record. It carries on and is marked partial, with the reason. Changing the mode keeps what was counted so far.
- **An overlay over the game.** It shows what's under way, then the result for a few seconds after. It never takes the game's focus. Drag it anywhere and it stays there, or make it click-through. Right-click it to change the mode. Turn it on or off on the Parser tab or with Ctrl+Shift+P.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
