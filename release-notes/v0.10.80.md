Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.80: a Parser for boss fights, whole runs or every fight.

- **A new Parser tab, in three modes.** The tab names what each mode counts:
  - **Bosses only** (the default): each boss pull in Transcendence, Expeditions and Sanctuaries. Trash and the open world never count, and every new boss starts fresh.
  - **Full run**: each run of those, from entering to leaving, trash included. Every boss is a split with its time and result, and a run is cleared when its final boss dies.
  - **Every fight**: fights anywhere, the open world and the Abyss included. Each lasts from the first hit or kill to 20 seconds without one, with your kills (players counted apart) and your deaths.
- **Live, in real time.** Every hit shows on the tab and the overlay as it lands, in well under a tenth of a second. You see the boss or the run with its mode ("Ferocious Horn Den · Conquest"), a running clock, the boss's HP, and each member's DPS and share of the damage ("3,851 DPS · 31.0%"); rest the pointer on a row for its damage. Damage and DPS are never estimated.
- **DPS your way.** DPS is over the fight's whole length by default. Settings › In game › Parser › DPS over active time counts each member over the time they were dealing damage instead.
- **Each player's class, at a glance.** Every member shows the game's own class emblem, read from the party list, and their row sits on a meter bar in the game's colour for the class, as long as their share of the damage. A member shows up once they deal damage.
- **Damage by ability.** Click a member to see their item level, Combat Power and server, then each of their abilities: total damage, hits, the average hit and its share.
- **Minimal by default.** Settings › In game › Parser adds each member's class, Combat Power, item level and damage, kills near you and the group's deaths, filters for the list, and explanations on hover.
- **Everything is kept on this PC, per character.** Past records are listed by mode; open one for its group, its splits, timings and kills. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Robust by design.** A lost connection, a reconnect or a tracker restart doesn't split a record. It carries on and is marked partial, with the reason. Changing the mode keeps what was counted so far.
- **Right in the Kinah overlay.** When a fight starts, the Parser opens above the Kinah line, which stays where it is, then folds away a few seconds after the result. It never takes the game's focus. Settings › In game › Parser › Separate parser overlay gives it a window of its own instead: press Move overlay on the Parser tab to place it with sample rows before any fight, drag it anywhere and it stays there, make it click-through, or right-click it to change the mode. Turn it on or off on the Parser tab or with Ctrl+Shift+P.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
