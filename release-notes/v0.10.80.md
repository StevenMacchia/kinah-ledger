Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.80: every gathering attempt, exactly.

- **Gathering counts from the game itself.** Each try on a node is counted the moment the game settles it: whether it worked, won the upgrade roll (the Fine or Pure grade) or failed, and exactly what it gave. Tries the screen used to miss, like the middle try on a node or an upgrade read as a plain success, are now counted right.
- **Nodes and tries are told apart.** A skill now says how many nodes you gathered and how many tries that took ("45 tries on 15 nodes"), with the upgrades won and the fails, the tries an hour and what the hour's gathering is worth.
- **Each kind of node gets its own line** under its skill, with its tries, nodes, upgrades, fails and pace.
- **The screen still checks.** If the game's messages stop agreeing with what the screen reads, the tracker goes back to counting from the screen, as it does for Kinah and loot.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
