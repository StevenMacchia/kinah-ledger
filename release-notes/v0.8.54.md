Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.54: the tracker's pictures can never take more than 4 GB of your drive.

- **One limit over all of the tracker's pictures.** The tracker keeps a few pictures on your PC: reward screens it reads runs from, the rare-drop crops, and miscount reports. Each kind already had its own limit and cleared itself every hour. Reports kept because they hadn't been sent yet had no size limit, though, and nothing capped everything together. Now, if all of them pass 4 GB, the oldest are deleted first, whatever kind they are. The folder can't pass 5 GB, even between two hourly clean-ups.
- Your settings, prices, history and icons are never touched by the clean-up.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
