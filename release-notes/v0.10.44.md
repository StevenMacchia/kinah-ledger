Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.44: more of the window moves when something changes.

- **Bag counts roll up.** When you pick up more of an item, its count in the bag strip and on the LOOT tiles rolls up to the new number, as the big figure does.
- **Bars slide.** The gold bar toward your best here, the crafting bar and the Next up bars slide to their new length instead of jumping.
- **A goal reached lights up.** When a goal or a quest in Next up is done, its row lights up once in its colour and settles.
- **The rate chart draws itself in.** Open "chart" on the Farming card and the line draws in from the left.

Each plays once per change and nothing loops, so the tracker costs the game nothing while it sits still. With Windows's animations turned off, none of it plays.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
