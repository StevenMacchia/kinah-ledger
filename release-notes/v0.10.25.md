Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.25: quests.

- **Quests.** The tracker sets goals from what you are farming and gives points for reaching them. Next up on the Farming page is the quest list: the round Kinah figure this session is about to pass on top, then two crafting materials from what you are farming (the kind you last picked up, then the most valuable other stack), and behind "N more" the other materials, gathering, the runs at each place today and beating your best run there. A rarer material is worth more, and a quest done today or this week more than one done this session.
- **Trees.** Only Next up shows. A click on a quest opens its tree: the next steps it unlocks and the same thing over today and this week, each with its points. Hover any of them for the facts.
- **Done, and your score.** A quest just reached wears a mint check and its points for a few minutes, then sits in the Done fold at the foot of the card; the tracker says it once on the panel and the status line, no sound. Your score, this session, today and this week, is on Next up's head and in Options › Sharing. It stays on this PC for now; a coming version sends it to your profile on kinahledger.com once your tracker is linked, for the leaderboards.
- **Expeditions and events, Crafting.** The place's box gets the runs here today and beating your best run here; the Crafting page's Next up the two materials you are farming.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
