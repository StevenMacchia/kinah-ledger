Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.25: keep the window above others, Ctrl+1/2/3 for the pages, Save as CSV for crafts and the week.

- **Keep above other windows.** A switch in Options › General and in the ⋯ menu: the window stays over the game and everything else, for a windowed game on one screen. Off by default; the panel over the game is on top either way.
- **Ctrl+1, 2, 3 switch pages.** Farming, Crafting, Events as the tabs sit, the keypad's 1 2 3 too. Nothing happens before a server is chosen. The shortcut list in Options shows them.
- **Save as CSV for the crafts.** On the Crafting page, beside Start over: This session's recipes as a file for a spreadsheet, with tries, how many worked, what came out, cost, what it sells for after the Market's cut and the profit.
- **Save this week as CSV.** In the ⋯ menu: a row a day for seven days, with what farming made, the Kinah, time spent, kills, zones, and the runs with their Kinah and places. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Names for the icon buttons.** The site and the Compact/Full view buttons now have names for screen readers.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
