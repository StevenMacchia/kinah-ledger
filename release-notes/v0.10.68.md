Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.68: item pickups and crafts counted from the game's own messages.

- **Item pickups come from the game itself.** With Npcap installed, the tracker now counts the items you pick up from the game server's messages, the way it already counts Kinah: each pickup, with its exact count, even when the chat line scrolled past unread. In a 3-minute Abyss test the chat had missed a Lesser Manastone and some Fresh Raw Meat; the game's messages had both. A pickup line the game's messages didn't show still counts from the chat, and the chat takes over again if the two stop agreeing.
- **Crafts come from the game too.** While you craft, each craft counts from the game's messages: what it made, the won combos (a dish's x2), and the tries that made nothing. On a queue of 7 Kukuru Fried Meat, the crafting panel's bars had counted 6, missing one that finished in 2 seconds; the game's messages had all 7, two of them combos, 9 dishes in all. The panel counts again whenever the game's messages can't.
- **Only pickups count.** As with Kinah, an item that arrives with no kill, gather or pickup around it, in a base, with the Market or a reward screen open, or bound with no pickup line for it (a quest's or a box's) isn't farming, and a craft's item is never a pickup.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
