Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.58: one update prompt instead of two.

- **No more update pop-up.** When a new version was ready, the tracker showed a Windows box asking "Install it now?" on top of the banner that already said so. The box is gone. The banner at the top of the window is the one place it asks, with Restart now to install at once. Otherwise it installs when you close the tracker.
- **Still easy to notice.** While you play, the in-game panel says "Update ready · installs when you close the tracker" for a minute. If the tracker is minimized or in the tray, a Windows notification says it once. Nothing takes focus from the game.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
