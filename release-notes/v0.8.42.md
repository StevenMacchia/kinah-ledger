Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.42: rare drops show their price and can chime, a "That was wrong" shortcut, and a guide to place the panel.

- **Rare drops tell you what they're worth.** A Legend, Unique or Epic drop now shows its icon in its grade frame, the name in the grade's colour and "sells for 28K on Siel" (or "no price yet") on the Card and the Ticker for the 5-second hold. The Strip adds the price to its one-line alert. While you craft, the Card keeps your crafting figures and only its border lights up.
- **A watch list and a chime.** Options › In game › Rare drops has a watch list: items on it always alert, whatever their grade, and show a gold "watching" tag. Add them with the Add item… box or "Alert me when it drops" on an item card; items you already watched carry over. The chime has a switch, a volume slider and Play. Price alerts chime at the same volume.
- **"That was wrong" shortcut.** Pick a key in Options › In game, under the start/stop shortcut (it is off until you do). Press it in the game when a count looks wrong: the newest line counted in the last 2 minutes is marked and the last minute of reads is saved as a report, with the game kept in front. The panel says "Saved the last minute · Report in tracker" for 3 seconds, and the window marks the line with "Undo" for 30 seconds.
- **Place the panel.** After the first setup check, a guide opens over the game once: drag the panel, or snap it to Top centre, Under minimap or Left of target, then Enter to keep it or Esc to put it back. The Card and the Ticker each keep their own spot. Open it again from Options › In game › Place the panel… or the tray menu.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
