Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.41: opening the Market to buy reads its prices.

- **The Market is read when you open an item to buy it.** The tracker only read the Market's main page (each item with its lowest price). When you searched an item and opened its listings to buy, it read nothing, so nothing reached the site and the tracker didn't show you were at the Market. It now reads those listings too: each one's price and stack, under the item's name. A listing is shared only when its price is within 3x of the item's price on the board, so a misread number never goes out.
- **The Market's notes in the log.** The tracker's log now says when it found the Market, how many prices it read for which items, and what the site took. If the Market is open but its prices can't be read, the tracker keeps one picture of the game window on this PC (in the market-shots folder, the newest 6) so the next fix can see the screen. Nothing in that folder is ever sent anywhere.
- **Self-tests stay out of your log.** The Market self-test no longer writes its made-up prices into your own log.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
