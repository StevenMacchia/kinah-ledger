Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.51: one row per recipe in Crafting, and each page's Today line shows its own day.

- **One row per recipe.** A crafting window title read slightly wrong ("Mana Potion (Novice I-v-40)1", "tvQewa Potion") no longer becomes a recipe of its own. Those crafts now land on the real recipe, with its price and profit.
- **Farming shows today's farming.** On the Farming page, the Today line and its slots show only farming: time farming, kills, drops from kills and what you gathered, with what that made. Zone picks work as before.
- **Events shows today's expeditions and events.** On the Events page, the Today line shows how many expeditions and events you ran today, and opens to their rewards and what they made. No farming loot there.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
