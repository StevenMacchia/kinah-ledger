Kinah Ledger tracker: the Parser stays up through the whole dungeon.

- **In a dungeon the overlay is your meter.** In Transcendence, Expeditions and Sanctuaries the meter no longer folds away after a fight. It shows the fight under way, then keeps that fight's result on screen until the next pull starts it afresh. Before the first pull it shows the dungeon's name, ready for it. In the open world your DPS stays one small figure on the Kinah line, as before.
- **No "Other" row.** Every row is a member of your group, and their shares of the damage add up to 100%.
- **Bigger, steadier figures.** Names, DPS, shares and the clock are larger in every overlay look and on the Parser tab, and the digits keep their width, so the rows don't twitch as hits land.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
