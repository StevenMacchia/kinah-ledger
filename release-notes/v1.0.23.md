Kinah Ledger 1.0.23: the Parser page no longer goes blank at the start of a pull.

- **The Parser page shows a pull from the first hit.** When a boss had taken damage but nobody in your group had landed a hit yet, the page could stay empty instead of showing your group. It now shows the group straight away, and the damage fills in as it lands.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
