Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.50: see everything you got today, farming and runs, and All zones that stays put.

- **Today, above Farming and Events.** A gold line at the top of the window shows your day so far: time farming, kills, runs, and what it all made. Click it to open today's loot as inventory slots, with farming and expeditions and events shown separately. Hover a slot to see where it dropped, how often, and what it's worth.
- **Every zone, or one.** Today covers every zone by default, so it doesn't start over when you move between zones. Click a zone to see only that zone's loot. The pick stays on that zone while you move around.
- **Kept across restarts.** Today runs from midnight on your PC. Closing the tracker, an update or a reboot doesn't reset it.
- **All zones keeps counting.** The All zones option in the ⋯ menu started over by itself every 15 minutes. It now counts every zone from when the tracker started, or from the start of your run.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
