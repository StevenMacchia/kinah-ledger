Kinah Ledger 1.0.17: the app tells you why the DPS meter is off, and pop-out windows for a second monitor.

- **Why is my meter missing?** If something keeps the DPS meter from working, the app now says what and how to fix it: Npcap not installed (with a Get Npcap button, and the meter starts on its own once it is in), Npcap set to administrators only, the game in exclusive fullscreen (set Display mode to Borderless), a ping booster or VPN hiding the game, or reading switched off.
- **Pop-out windows.** The DPS meter and the Applicants list can each open in their own window, for a second monitor. They keep working while the game is in exclusive fullscreen. Open them from the Overlays menu, the Parser page or Settings.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
