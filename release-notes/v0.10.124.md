Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.124: a bigger heal bar you can switch off, and your records kept per fight.

- **The heal bar is back, and bigger.** A green bar along each healer's row shows their share of the group's healing. It's twice as thick as before. Turn it off in Settings › In game › Parser › Heal bars. The HEAL chip after the name stays either way.
- **Records are per fight.** Your best DPS, best healing a second, fastest kill and biggest hit are kept for each boss in each instance and mode. Necromancer Duanka in Conquest and in Expedition are two records. They're saved for each character and outlive old fights. The first start builds them from every fight you've kept.
- **Record moments compare against the same fight only.** The PB chip after a kill measures you against your record on that boss, in that instance and mode, never the same boss somewhere else. The pace glow during the fight does too. A new healing record shows as "HEAL PB +9%", or beside the DPS one ("PB +4.2% · HEAL +9%").

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
