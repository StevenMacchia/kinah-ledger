Kinah Ledger 1.0.10: your best drop and last pickups on every overlay look.

- **Pickup icons on every look.** Next to your hour figure, the Kinah overlay shows your best drop (with a gold star) and your last three pickups as item icons, with the newest lit for a moment. The Buddy already had them; now every theme does, in its own style.
- **Turn them off if you like.** Settings › Buddy › More › Pickup icons. Off only hides the icons: every pickup is still counted, saved and sent to your profile.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
