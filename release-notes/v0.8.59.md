Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.59: half-read names no longer turn into the wrong item.

- **No more phantom Ink (Bound).** Long names scroll past in the loot list, so a read often catches only the end, like "…rubiel (Bound)" from an Eternal Kerubiel's soul. The tracker matched those pieces to the closest item it knew. It also compared new reads with earlier misreads, so each match drifted a letter or two, until dozens of different pieces were all counted as Ink (Bound). Now a read only matches a real item's name. A short name like Ink (Bound) needs a full read with at most one letter wrong.
- **Wisdom Stone is its own item.** It was missing from the item list, so it was read as Wisdom Staff, a Unique weapon. It's now known by its own name.
- Found with the new reading reports: trackers that share runs now also send which names they misread. That's how these showed up.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
