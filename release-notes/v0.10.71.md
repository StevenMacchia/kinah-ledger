Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.71: the Abyss counts as the Abyss.

- **Every Abyss area is one zone, "Abyss".** Sulfur Tree Island, Devastated Remnants, Black Fragments Area, Archipelago of Siel's Wings, Latesran's roots and ridges and the rest of Lower, Middle and Upper Reshanta now count together under Abyss. Moving from one area to the next no longer starts anything over. Before this, the tracker knew only names with "Reshanta" in them, so in most of the Abyss your Abyss Points and Kinah counted nowhere.
- **Abyss Points show again for Abyss farming,** with their rate an hour as the big figure, Kinah and loot an hour on the ticket under it.
- **The Kinah your Abyss kills drop counts.** Every kill's Kina is bound in the Abyss; it is now that kill's Kinah, in the count, Kinah an hour, Kinah a kill and Today. A big bound sum (a quest's, a reward's) is still set aside, as it is everywhere.
- **Drops in the Abyss count again.** Each kill's bound Kina line was being taken for a quest payout, so the gear, Spiritstones and soul crystals that dropped beside it were set aside as the payout's. In a minute of Steven's Abyss farming that was four drops and ten soul crystals; all of them count now.
- **Destroyed Abyss Gate is Verteron,** not the Abyss, as the game's map has it.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
