Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.28: counting is more careful, and Shugo event rewards count.

- Kina lines are checked against your Kinah counter. A line the counter did not rise for (read twice, or pushed up by a scroll) is not counted again; a rise with no line is counted once.
- Loot-list rows are tracked by where they are in the list and their icon, not by their text, so a row that moves up or scrolls its long name is one pickup.
- Your own title, nameplates and zone text over the loot list are no longer mistaken for rows.
- Garbled item names are matched against what can drop where you are, and a read that fits nothing nearby is left out rather than guessed. Lost quantities are recovered from the loot list before "1" is assumed.
- Shugo Festival reward cards are read from the picture and filed as the run's rewards, Abyss Points included. Only Hidden Lugi has been seen so far; other events keep their pictures until their layout is known.
- A run whose rewards could not be read is kept, not dropped, and can be recovered later from its saved pictures.
- In reward tables, gear counts one piece; a Daeva Pass reward screen no longer opens a false run; very old or merged run records are flagged and left out of averages.
- The counting delay after a pickup is shorter, and the tracker logs where its time goes once a minute.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
