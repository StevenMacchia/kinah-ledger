Kinah Ledger tracker 0.7.3. Download KinahLedger.exe and run it; nothing to install.

- A new logo: the gold K, on the exe, the window, the taskbar and the tray.
- First run asks about sharing up front: one switch, on by default, with What's shared and Privacy beside it. Options › Sharing still changes it any time.
- What's shared now also lists the Market prices the tracker sends while the Market is open.
- Properties on the exe shows the version and a copyright line.

Settings, history and logs are kept. Trackers on 0.7.2 update by themselves.
