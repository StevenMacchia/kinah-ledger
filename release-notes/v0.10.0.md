Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.0: the app says why it counted what it counted.

- **Every counted line can explain itself.** Click a craft, a drop or a Kinah line and its card says why it was counted, in the reads log's words: "success bar 99%, fail bar 84%", "the success bar filled in an instant (a critical success); the queue's count passed it", "the x2 pop-up said the combo was won: the second dish credited". Lines without a note of their own show the notes written around their time. A recipe's card lists its last five tries the same way.
- **A craft queue shows its progress as it runs.** The panel, the Ticker and the Crafting page carry "3 of 10 · 2 worked · 1 combo · 1 unsure" while the queue runs, and "Done: 10 of 10 · 8 worked · 2 combos" for a while after, so a miss shows while it happens.
- **Three sounds, not one.** A watched or rare drop keeps its rising chime; a price alert is a knock, the same note twice; an update ready to install is three soft rising notes, and never plays during a run. Options › In game plays each one.
- **A recap when a run ends.** Time, kills, Kinah, loot after the Market's cut, the best drop and your best here, with Share a click away. It waits until the window is in front, so nothing draws over the game; off in Options › General.
- **A first-session checklist** replaces the empty feed on a new install: server picked, Aion 2 open, chat found, Market read once, panel placed, first run saved, each with the link that does it. Players with a run saved never see it.
- **Options › Shortcuts & tips** lists the keys, where each one works, and eight things that are easy to miss. The tray no longer shows Ctrl+N and Ctrl+Shift+C beside its items: those keys work in the window, not from the tray.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
