Kinah Ledger tracker 0.7.7. Trackers on 0.7.2 and later update by themselves; otherwise download KinahLedger.exe and run it.

**More reliable kill counts**
- Every kill's Soul Points line names the enemy that died. Kinah by enemy now credits each kill to that enemy, so several enemies fought at once all show, even when the HP bar is misread.
- Kills come from the evidence that agrees on them: a kill's Kinah, its Soul Points line and a Defeated line count as one kill, and a kill still counts when one of them is lost.
- The loot list beside your character checks the chat: an amount the chat misread by one digit is the same pickup, and a Kinah line the chat read twice counts once when the list showed it once.
- The loot list is read at the same time as the chat, so fast fights are read more often.
- A HP bar name cut short is its one enemy ("Drana Processing Mainte").

**In the window**
- Expeditions and events have their own tab and never show on the Farming page.

Settings, history and logs are kept.
