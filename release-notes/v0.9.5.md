Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.5: Shugo Festival and Dimensional Invasion runs count on the Events page.

- **Event runs with cut-off rewards now count.** The last row of a reward card often sits under the edge of its scroll window, so a few rewards can't be read. Those runs used to drop out of the Events page, Today, Goals and History. They now count with everything that was read; the rewards that couldn't be read stay unnamed, never guessed.
- **Cards picked more than once.** A card picked two to four times was already counted that many times, and now those runs show up in your totals too.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings, goals and history are kept.
