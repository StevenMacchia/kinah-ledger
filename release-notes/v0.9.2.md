Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.2: miscount reports reach us, and goals are easier to find.

- **Report a miscount now reaches us.** When a count looks wrong, Report a miscount sends what the tracker read and counted to Kinah Ledger so we can fix it. Chat from other players and anything with a character name is left out, and no pictures are sent. A copy still stays on your PC, and if the site can't be reached the report waits and tries again later.
- **No more "to look at" pill.** The footer, the panel over the game and the Accuracy card no longer show a count of things to check. Options › Show accuracy details still brings the details back if you want them.
- **Set a goal from the Farming page.** With no goal set, a one-line GOALS row sits above LOOT. Click it to set a goal for today.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings, goals and history are kept.
