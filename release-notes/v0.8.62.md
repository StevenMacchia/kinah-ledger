Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.62: Shugo Festival rewards no longer get mixed up with a Daeva Pass reward.

- **A Daeva Pass reward in an event's lobby isn't the event's.** If your Daeva Pass levelled up while you waited for an event to start, the tracker took the pass's reward for the event's payout. It then kept pictures of the lobby as if they were the reward screen, and read them again with the real reward cards. That could make the event's Abyss Points come out wrong (780 instead of 1,100 on one event). Daeva Pass, attendance, achievement, shop and mail rewards now stay out of an open event.
- Today's Defend Shugo Merchants reward screen is now a saved test, so the tracker is checked against it on every release.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
