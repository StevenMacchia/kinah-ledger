Kinah Ledger 1.0.28: boss pulls count after a restart mid-run, and a lighter Timers page.

- **Boss pulls after an update or restart in the middle of a run.** If the tracker restarted while you were inside a dungeon (for example after pressing Restart to update), it didn't know where you were until the next loading screen: the bosses you fought until then were open-world fights, not kept on the Parser page or in your personal bests. It now knows the run within a couple of seconds of starting, so those pulls are kept as they were, with your whole group from their first hit.
- **The Timers page stays light.** While it was open it rebuilt every row once a second; now only the countdowns tick, so it no longer keeps your PC busy.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
