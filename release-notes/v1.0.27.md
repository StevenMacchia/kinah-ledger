Kinah Ledger 1.0.27: your Odyle Energy on the DPS meter.

- **Odyle Energy on the meter.** A tiny Odyle Energy icon and your character's energy now sit after the total in the meter's foot, in every look, on the overlay and in the meter's own window. It changes when the game changes it (a run's final reward, an Odyle Energy item, the refill every three hours), so it is right after each run.
- **Each character keeps its own.** Switch characters and the meter shows that character's energy. After a restart it shows the last figure it knew, counting the refills since, until a fresh one comes. Before the tracker has ever seen it, nothing shows (never a 0).
- **Off if you'd rather not see it.** Settings › DPS meter › More › Odyle Energy. It never goes on the meter's share picture.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
