Kinah Ledger 1.0.2: every drop counted, and a picture of your DPS meter to share.

- **Bound drops count.** A bound item dropped in the Abyss (an Artisan's Refining Stone, Raw Leather, a Spiritstone, a Superior Manastone) was sometimes missed. It now counts once, on the run and day lists and in the rare-drop pop-up, as bound with no value.
- **Drops with no kill seen.** In the Abyss the game doesn't always say who made a kill, and drops from those kills were set aside. They count now.
- **No more double counts from dismantling.** Gear auto-dismantled into Enhance Stones counted the stones as a drop on top of the gear.
- **Share your DPS meter.** Hover the meter (with click-through off) and press Share, use the Share button on the Parser page, or set a Share picture shortcut in Options › DPS meter. The meter is copied as a picture in its look, ready to paste in Discord or anywhere, and saved in Pictures › Kinah Ledger. Other players' names are hidden by default (Hide other names).

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
