Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.56: a calmer Farming page.

- **One loot box, one period at a time.** Under the zone card, Loot shows what you picked up as inventory slots, with one total. Switch it between This zone and Today. The Today line at the top and the Session line are gone: they were the same numbers counted over different stretches, and it was hard to tell which was yours.
- **Hover a slot** for its odds, how many come an hour and what it's worth. Click it for the full card, right-click to report it.
- **Time reads one way:** 5 min, 1 h 12 min. Zones you only passed through get no chip.
- **Quieter.** "Reading the chat", skipped reward lines and the last line read now wait in the ⋯ menu. A reading problem still shows on the page, with its fix. Where to farm now shows once it has a zone to suggest.
- **Full view** keeps the drop tables beside the loot, as before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
