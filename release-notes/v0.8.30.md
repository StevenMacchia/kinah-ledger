Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.30: see what was just counted, and how sure it is.

- Every tab now opens on the last five things the tracker counted, with the rate beside them. If a line is wrong or missing, tap it to report it with one click.
- A new card says how much of your session the game's own numbers confirmed, and lists anything it was unsure about.
- Shugo Festival reward cards that were only partly on screen now count what was seen, say "Partly read", and still reach your profile.
- Every gathering attempt counts, named by the node you were on, including mining and cooking; a gather the tracker did not watch is counted from its result.
- A chat line that scrolls back into view is no longer counted twice.
- Gear counts that an older build inflated are repaired on your PC and corrected on the site.
- Help improve counting: confirmed reads are shared as small data records so counting gets better for everyone. On by default, named in Options, one click to turn off. No chat text and no player names ever leave your PC.
- The Events card lists runs as time, length, Kinah and pieces; a record that looks like several runs merged can be split, kept or removed.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
