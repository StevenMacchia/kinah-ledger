Kinah Ledger 1.0.38: a timeline of every boss pull, cast by cast.

- **The Timeline tab.** Open a boss pull on the Parser page and pick Timeline, next to Compare. Each of your abilities gets its own lane, biggest first: a mark for every cast as it landed and a band for the damage it did each second. The smaller abilities fold into one lane; Show all opens them.
- **Zoom in on any moment.** Scroll to zoom, drag to move, double-click (or Fit) for the whole pull. Hover any mark for the exact event: a cast's time, damage and hits, your biggest hits, the group's damage spikes, the boss's HP and the moments it crossed 75, 50 and 25%.
- **Deaths with a recap.** Every death in the group is marked across the timeline. Click one to see the last hits that person took, with times, amounts, who dealt them and the killing blow.
- **Real data only.** Each lane adds up to the pull's own figure for that ability, and the deaths are the fight's own. Pulls kept before this version have no timeline; your next boss pull will.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
