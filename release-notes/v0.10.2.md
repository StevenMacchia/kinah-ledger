Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.2: the Crafting tab counts what came out.

- **A ×10 craft shows as ten.** A recipe's row on the Crafting tab now says what came out under its name when that isn't one item a craft: "15 made · 1 craft of ×10" for one craft of the ×10 Orichalcum Ingot and five of the ×1, which share a row. The session line says it too, "6 crafts · 6 worked · 15 made", and so does the row's tooltip. Before, the ×10 craft was one in the Crafts column and the ten showed only on its feed line.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
