Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.42: the window moves when something comes in.

- **Figures roll to their new value.** When a pickup changes your hour, the Kinah and loot chips, the bag's total or the day's total, the figure rolls up to its new value in under half a second, and a rise in money flashes green for a moment. The hour no longer jumps as the clock ticks; it moves only when something is counted.
- **Drops land.** A new item in the bag or in Loot grows in as it appears, and more of one swells for a moment. A Legend, Unique or Epic drop glows once in its grade's colour.
- **Sections open smoothly.** The page's sections and the groups on the Runs page slide open and fold away, and their arrow turns with them.

Nothing moves unless something changed, so the tracker costs your game nothing while it sits still. With Windows's animations turned off (Settings, Accessibility, Visual effects), it all stays still.

Also in this update (from 0.10.41, which wasn't released on its own):

- **The Market is read when you open an item to buy it.** The tracker used to read only the Market's main page. It now also reads the listings you open to buy, each one's price and stack, and shares a listing only when its price is within 3x of the item's price on the board, so a misread number never goes out.
- **The Market's notes in the log.** The log says when the tracker found the Market and how many prices it read. If the prices can't be read, it keeps one picture of the game window on this PC (in the market-shots folder, the newest 6) for the next fix. Nothing in that folder is ever sent anywhere.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
