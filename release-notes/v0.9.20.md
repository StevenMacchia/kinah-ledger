Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.20: a craft that finishes in an instant counts.

- **A critical success that completes at once is a craft.** Its Success bar fills in under two seconds, which the tracker took for the flicker a panel shows as it opens, and dropped; the next craft then read "1 craft before it not seen". The two tell apart by the queue's count: it passes a real craft, never a flicker. A quick success is now kept, and when the queue's count passes a craft since the last one, it is counted as that craft, at its own time; the last craft of a queue the same when the queue ends.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
