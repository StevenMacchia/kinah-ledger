Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.8: world bosses.

- When a world boss is on the top enemy bar, nothing counts toward farming until it dies: the adds dying around it, their Kinah and their drops are set aside, so Kinah/hr and kills stay honest.
- When the boss dies, what you pick up in the next 30 seconds is its loot: a run of its own on the Events tab, never part of farming.
- Other players' shares of a Force's loot ("Name acquired … through Force distribution") are never yours, and never a kill.
- While a Force hands out loot, the panel beside your character shows the boss's loot table; those rows count only when the chat says the item is yours.
- Your own share ("Acquired … through Force distribution") is now read as the item.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
