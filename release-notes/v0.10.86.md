Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.86: place the timers overlay any time.

- **Move overlay.** On the Timers page and in Settings › Timers, Move overlay shows the timers strip right away so you can drag it where you want it, even when no timer is due. Done or Esc keeps the spot, and Reset position puts it back at the top right.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
