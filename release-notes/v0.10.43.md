Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.43: rewards stay rewards, the hour keeps going across zones, and an Always on top box.

- **The hour no longer starts over in every zone.** The big figure and the tables now count every zone you farm until you take a break (15 minutes with nothing farmed), so changing zones keeps your hour going. Going into the Abyss, or back out to the world, starts it over, since that's another kind of farming. Click the big figure to see this zone only, and click it again to go back. Overall is the default, and the label beside the figure says which one you're looking at.
- **Always on top, where you can see it.** An "Always on top" box sits at the bottom right of the window, so the tracker stays over the game. It's the same switch as Options › General and the ⋯ menu, and it stays off until you tick it.
- **Rewards claimed during a run stay rewards.** When the Daeva Pass Level Reward window opened over an event's rewards, it covered the chat for a moment, and the tickets it gave (Unknown Fissure Challenge Ticket) were counted as the event's loot and as farming loot. Anything a pass, shop, attendance or achievement window gives is now filed as that window's reward, inside a run or out in the world, even when the chat was garbled as the window opened.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
