Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.34: clearer signs on the panel and the first screen.

- **Badge shows what kind of line it is.** Each line under the figures now starts with a small sign: a gold "i" for an update or a price alert, a spark in the drop's colour for a rare drop or a goal, a check or a warning for the accuracy, the red dot when a run starts and a stop square when it ends. "Run stopped" reads as the end of the run, with what it made in gold.
- **Each shortcut is set in one place.** Start and stop a run and That was wrong are set on Options › Shortcuts & tips. In game keeps a button that takes you there.
- **The welcome screen says what goes in the gear score box** (the number on your character window, e.g. 1450) and who your runs are compared with: players in the same band of 200. A last line says what to do after Continue: open Aion 2, keep the chat on screen, and press Start run.
- **No more letters in place of item pictures.** An item without a picture shows a small sign for what it is (a sack for loot, a shield for gear, a coin for a currency, a leaf for something gathered) instead of its first letter.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
