Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.76: Rank Points in the Abyss.

- **Rank Points show in the Abyss.** The line under the big figure now reads, for example, "24,740 AP · 2,104 Rank · 266.5K Kinah · 77,872 loot · 9 PvP · 109 mobs". A new ticket beside "Kinah and loot an hour" gives Rank Points an hour.
- **What deaths cost.** Hover the Rank figure or its ticket to see the Rank Points lost on deaths and how many you kept.
- **Counted from the game itself.** With Npcap, every kill's Rank Points come from the game's own messages, including the kills whose chat line scrolled past. Without it they count from the chat's Rank Point lines, as before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
