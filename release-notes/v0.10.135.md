Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.135: gold and blue gear from reward tables counted, and quests sync again.

- **Gold and blue gear gets named.** A reward table's gold (Unique) and blue (Legend) slots have a gradient behind the item art. The reader used to compare each icon against one flat color, so right answers like Nuakum Pauldrons, Fera Necklace and Black Claw Earrings sat just past its "sure" mark and were left unread. It now follows the slot's real background. It also names a slot when one item is clearly closer than every other match. Genuine coin-flips are still left unread, never guessed.
- **Balaur's Essence (Bound) is counted.** It's in almost every expedition's table, and it went unread every time because its art in the table didn't match the item list. Now it's recognized.
- **Your past runs are fixed too.** On first start, the tracker reads past expedition runs that had unread slots again from their kept reward pictures. Each run keeps everything it already had and only adds what's now named. On this PC, that's 54 runs and 21 pieces of gear. Runs whose pictures are gone (older than about a week) stay as they were. Once the site accepts corrections that add items, the fixed runs go to your profile.
- **Quests sync again.** Completed quests only went to the site with farming uploads, so a day of dungeons sent none. Your profile has had nothing since Nov 13, while about 1,700 completions waited on this PC. Now they go with every upload, 50 at a time (what the site keeps per upload), and on their own every 10 minutes while some are waiting. They're marked sent only once the site takes them, and switching character no longer loses track of them. The backlog drains by itself, and each day still gets credit for its own quests.
- **One id per piece of gear.** Reward gear is bound to your character, so it's now always filed under the gear's bound id, the same one the chat uses.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
