Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.33: every item has a price.

- **A single listing counts.** An item your server lists once (Inina, one listing at 5,550 on Siel) now adds to the loot total and the value per hour, marked with "~". So does a price last seen over a day ago. Before, these showed dimmed and added nothing.
- **Asks the site set aside still count.** When the site leaves your server's asks out because they sit far above the other servers (Inina Pasta, two listings at 8,500 on Siel), the tracker uses the lowest of them, marked "~", instead of showing no price at all.
- **Nothing on your server? The other servers fill in.** An item with no listing on your server is priced at the other servers' middle, marked "≈". It never sets a craft's cost: a material your server doesn't list still asks you to type what you'd pay, so an Intermediate Manastone craft can't cost 400K again.
- **Crafts use every price on your server.** A one-listing or day-old material price now counts in the craft's cost, and the Crafting card flags it as a thin market when it decides the profit.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
