Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.112: one-hit fights join the fight before.

- **No more one-hit spikes.** In the open world, a mob you kill with a single hit no longer becomes a fight of its own on the meter. It joins the fight before it: its damage is added, and its second is added to that fight's time. The quiet between them doesn't count. The meter then holds your real pace, not the one hit divided by a second.
- **Real fights stay separate.** A fight with several hits is still its own fight, starting from 0.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
