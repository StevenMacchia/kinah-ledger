Kinah Ledger 1.0.44: two new themes, Esports and Elements, with eight looks between them.

- **Esports, a new theme.** A tournament on the stream, for the Kinah overlay and the DPS meter.
  - **Broadcast:** slanted lower-third plates, a red LIVE tag, a branded stinger on every pull, a NEW LEADER ticker, and a VICTORY card with the MVP on the kill.
  - **Telestrator:** an analyst's replay board, with the caster's felt-tip circling the leader and drawing arrows and calls live.
  - **Hype:** leaning chrome plates in electric lime and magenta, and every lead change a slam.
  - **Finals:** a grand final in black and gold under stage lights, a trophy over the leader, and CHAMPION and confetti on the kill.
- **Elements, a new theme.** The meter made of the elements.
  - **Frost:** a slab of glacial ice with crystal crowns and icicles; frost creeps along the bars, and a snowflake crown freezes onto the leader.
  - **Storm:** a thunderhead over storm glass with charged bars; a real thunderclap strikes the new leader.
  - **Magma:** cracked basalt over a lava river, every bar a molten flow; the fight erupts.
  - **Tide:** the deep, seen from under the surface; a breaking wave crowns the meter, every bar is a rising wave, and a pearl marks the leader.
- **Moments, not noise.** Each look plays its effects only when something happens and sits still while you read it. The figures never move, and nothing covers them.
- **Eight themes in Settings.** The Theme chips now sit in two even rows, so the Buddy and DPS meter pages still fit without scrolling.
- **Everywhere.** Every new look works in both windows, at any width and size, in Korean and Chinese, and with the PB ghost.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
