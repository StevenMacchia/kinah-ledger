Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.15: honest prices, and rewards that print with a payout stay rewards.

- Prices are honest now: your own Market read, or a price you type (click any price on a card), beats the site's. A price from a single listing is shown dimmed and left out of totals and profit, and every price says where it's from and how old it is.
- The Crafting column that read 330K for the Orichalcum Longsword was the cost of one try's materials. It is labelled COST / TRY now, and the Market is read for your own prices even with sharing off.
- A reward line the chat wraps in two ("Acquired Artisan's Ultimate Refining Stone" / "(Bound) x2.") counts as one reward of 2, not a drop.
- An item printed with a bound Kina payout (a quest, a pass or a claim) is the payout's reward, not a drop, and a reward no longer keeps the next kill's drop out.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
