Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.12: after an update, the window says what changed.

- **What's new, in the window.** After an update, a card at the top of the Farming page shows what changed in that version, a line each, like the ones you are reading now. Got it puts it away; All notes on GitHub opens the release.
- **Once per version.** Got it is remembered on this PC, so the card comes back only with the next update. A new install starts with the Welcome card instead.
- **Out of the way of the count.** The card sits above the live card on the Farming page only, in Compact and Full view, and the window gets its height back when it goes.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
