Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.47: the Market's search results are read.

- **Prices from the Market's Main tab.** When you search for an item on the Main tab, the Market lists every seller's stack with its price per item, the stack's price and the time left. The tracker didn't know those columns, so it said "nothing read yet" the whole time the Market was open. It now reads each listing there, by name.
- **The lowest price is the one that counts.** Of all the listings for an item, its lowest price per item is the price the tracker keeps and shares; the dearer ones aren't sent.
- **It looks again when the page changes.** Going from the Market's item list to an item's listings no longer leaves the tracker reading the old page for the rest of the visit.

Your own items for sale on the List tab are still left alone: your asking price isn't the Market's.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
