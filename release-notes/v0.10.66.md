Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.66: Kinah and Abyss Points counted from the game's own messages.

- **Kinah, bound Kinah and Abyss Points come from the game itself.** With Npcap installed (npcap.com), the tracker reads them from the game server's messages to your PC, exact to the Kinah, instead of from the chat. In a 3-minute Abyss test, the chat reading had missed about a third of the bound Kinah and Abyss Points; the game's messages had every one, along with Kinah the chat misread (519 read as 5,193) or never read. The chat still counts them when Npcap isn't there, and takes over again if the game's messages stop agreeing with it (after a patch, say).
- **The Abyss shows its Kinah.** Every Kinah drop in the Abyss is bound, and bound Kinah used to be left out, so the Abyss showed next to none. It now counts in the Abyss's Kinah figures. Outside the Abyss, bound Kinah is still left out as reward money.
- **Only pickups count.** A rise with no kill, gather or pickup around it (a sale to an NPC, the mail, a quest), one in a base, or one with the Market open isn't farming, and counts nothing.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
