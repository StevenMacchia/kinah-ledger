Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.74: one screen, numbers as they happen, and a Market buy is never a drop.

- **One screen.** The "This zone / All zones" switch is gone. The window counts every zone since your last break (15 minutes with nothing farmed), and each figure says how much of it came where you are now: "632.3K Kinah · 274.4K here", "an hour · 1.78M here". The share shows once you have farmed in more than one place. LOOT's first side is This session.
- **Zones stop switching under you.** A new zone counts only once you have been in it for a minute, or right away behind a loading screen or at a dungeon's door. Walking into the neighbouring zone and back, or a misread of the minimap, no longer starts anything over. An expedition's rooms are one place. On a replay of Steven's 13 November, the place changed 8 times an hour instead of 18, and the whole count started over at a zone change only on the 7 real trips into or out of the Abyss (19 times on the day before this, 10 of them in one hour).
- **Numbers from the game's messages show at once.** Kinah, bound Kinah and Abyss Points counted from the game's own messages used to wait 3 seconds before they counted, and the window then waited for its next refresh: 3.4 s on average. Now a pickup counts the moment it arrives when a kill was just seen (the game's own word that you killed something counts, so a fight's first pickup is instant too), and the window redraws right away. An item drop counts as soon as the chat has named it.
- **A bought item stays bought.** Two Wrathful Ego bought at the Market were set aside as bought, then counted as rare drops 23 seconds later: the crafting window had covered the chat, and when the chat showed again its first read had only one of the two lines, so the next reads took the lines for new, with Abyss kills beside them. Now a line the tracker set aside (a Market buy, a reward, the inventory at work) is remembered while the chat still shows it, so seeing it again after a window over the chat counts nothing. Replayed, that minute and a half counts no drop and no quest points.
- **The game's messages agree.** When the game's own messages count items, a line for an item they showed arriving but didn't count as a pickup (with the Market open, in a base, a reward screen up) never counts from the chat either, however late the chat shows it. Neither does the same line read twice, or a bought item's line when the game sent nothing for it.
- **Not a drop.** Click a pickup in the feed, or the item in your loot to open its card, and pick **Not a drop** on the pickup that was wrong. It leaves your totals: the farming count and the run, today's and the week's figures, the zone's and the enemy's drops, the rare-drop alert, and the quest points it gave. It's saved at once, so a restart keeps it. Undo (the bar, Ctrl+Z, or **Undo** on the item's card, the same day) puts it all back. After a restart, when the feed no longer has the pickup, click the item in LOOT's **Today** side: its card offers **Not a drop: take one out of today**, and an Undo for each one taken out today. **Looks wrong? Report** is unchanged: it keeps an item off the site and saves a report.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
