Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.3: sort the loot slots by worth, amount or quality.

- **Sort worth · amount · quality.** A small Sort line sits over the loot slots: on LOOT's This zone and Today sides on the Farming page, and in the TODAY strip's heading on the Events page. Worth is what you had, the most valuable first. Amount puts the most picked up first; quality the best grade first (Epic, Unique, Legend, Rare, then the rest), worth breaking ties. One choice for every side, kept between runs of the tracker.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
