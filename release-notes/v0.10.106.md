Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.106: the meter shows your combat DPS.

- **Combat DPS.** Outside boss pulls and runs (the open world, the Abyss, Every fight, and the meter Always on), DPS is your damage over the time you were fighting. The walk to the next pack no longer counts, so the figure stops sliding toward 0 between fights. A pause in your hits counts up to 5 seconds, so the meter eases for a few seconds after your last hit, then holds until you fight again.
- **Boss pulls and runs** are unchanged: a boss fight is all combat, timed from the pull to the kill or wipe.
- **No 1-second spikes.** The small DPS on the Kinah line is never worked out over less than 3 seconds, so a mob killed in a second no longer reads 200K a second.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
