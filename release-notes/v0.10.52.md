Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.52: Buddy moves too.

- **Buddy's figures roll.** On the Buddy overlay, the hour and the session roll up to their new figures when you pick something up, the hour turning green for a moment, and the card's edge lights gold.
- **The ring sweeps.** The ring of your best here sweeps round to its new share, its percent counting with it, and pulses once in mint the moment you pass your best.
- **Pickups land.** The newest pickup's icon grows in (a Legend, Unique, Epic or Special one glows once) and its name and worth pop in the card's top line.
- **Rare drops beat.** A rare drop's coloured frame beats twice as it lands.

Each plays once when something changes, and nothing loops. With Windows's animations turned off, none of it plays.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
