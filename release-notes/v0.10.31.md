Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.31: a manastone or soulstone combo makes the next grade.

- **A won combo on Intermediate Soulstone makes a Superior Soulstone.** The tracker used to count it as two Intermediate Soulstones. The same goes for Intermediate Manastone (Superior Manastone) and the Abyssal ones (Superior Abyssal Soulstone and Manastone).
- **Crafting profit counts the upgrade.** The Crafting card and the HUD now value a won combo on these stones at the Superior stone's price, and the card shows the chance of the upgrade.
- **The panel's title tells the roll.** When the crafting panel shows the Superior stone's name for the combo roll, the tracker takes that as the roll, as it already did for gear.
- **Everything else is unchanged.** Gear still upgrades to its Splendent piece. Dishes, potions and serums still make two, including Wind Serum and Life Serum, which have Superior recipes of their own.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
