Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.48: Daeva Pass rewards stay out of your loot, even when the window is quick.

- **Pass rewards never land in the bag.** When the Daeva Pass Level Reward window opens, it covers the chat for a moment. The tracker read the window's title a fraction of a second after the chat came back, so a Battle Enhance Scroll and an Odyle Energy from the pass landed in the bag as gathered loot. It now knows the pass window from its own words where the chat was, so everything the pass gives is filed as its reward from the moment it opens.
- **A gather stays a gather when a reward window pops up.** If the pass window (or any reward window) opened while you were gathering, the ore or herb your gathering bar had just produced could be filed as the window's reward. The tracker had already worked out it was a gather; now it counts as one.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
