Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.141: the whole tracker in Korean and Traditional Chinese.

- **Every page in your language.** Since 0.10.125 only the meter and the Parser spoke Korean (한국어) and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文). Now the rest does too: Farming, Crafting, Events, Quests and Timers, the welcome cards, Options, the menus and notices, every Kinah overlay look and Parser theme, the timers overlay and window, the inventory window, the recap, Week and Day wrapped, and the share pictures. Pick it in Options › General › Language, or leave it on Windows' language.
- **The game's own names.** Classes and factions use the official Korean and Taiwanese names (검성 / 劍星, 천족 / 天族 …). Items, zones, bosses and skills stay as the global servers write them, which is also how the tracker reads them.
- **Fits the window.** Every page and overlay was drawn in both languages at 110% and checked for text that runs out of its space.
- **Kept that way.** A new check draws every view in both languages and fails on any English word left on screen, so new text can't ship untranslated.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
