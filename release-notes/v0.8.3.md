Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.3: kills go to the enemy you actually killed.

- Kinah by enemy reads the nameplates around your character: an enemy in a fight shows its name with a red health bar. When an enemy's bar disappears as its loot lands, the kill is that enemy's. Group fights and enemies that drop no soul are credited correctly, and a soul named for a beast (like "Slink") no longer turns a mercenary kill into a Slink.
- Fixed in 0.8.2: the loot list's Kinah row read without its amount ("Kina x1") was counted as an item called Kina. It is never counted now.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
