Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.53: your characters get their real names, and your history goes to your main.

- **Your character's name, read for you.** The tracker reads the name over your character's head (the line just above its small health bar) and shows it at the top of the window once it has read the same name a few times. Your other characters are named from the run pictures kept of them. A name you type yourself is never replaced.
- **Your history goes to your main.** 0.10.51 gave everything kept before characters (days, bests, goals, quest points) to whichever character you happened to be on when it updated. Now it goes to the character with the most runs in your pictures, and is joined with what that character has counted since: days side by side, quest points added up, the better best kept. This happens once, by itself, shortly after the update.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
