Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.32: crafting fixes.

- A great success no longer files your craft under a made-up recipe. On some great successes the crafting panel's close button was read with the recipe's name ("Fine Durable Balaur Horn (Professional Lv. 30) X"), which made a row with no prices. Those crafts now count under the real recipe.
- The crafting feed shows a figure for every craft it can price. When one of a recipe's materials has no price yet, the line shows the most it could have made in grey ("≤ +38K"), the same way the This session table does, instead of a blank.
- "1 material has no price yet" reads right.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
