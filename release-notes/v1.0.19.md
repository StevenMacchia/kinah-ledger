Kinah Ledger 1.0.19: one detailed fight graph that draws as you fight.

- **One graph for the whole fight.** Every player's DPS line in their class colour (yours in gold), the boss's HP with the moments it crossed two thirds and one third, deaths marked, and the kill or wipe at the end, with names and DPS labelled at the right.
- **It draws live.** The frame is set from your best kill of that boss and holds still while the lines draw forward; finished fights draw in from left to right when you open them.
- **The group under the graph.** The Parser page shows the group table straight under the graph, no extra click; rows still open each player.
- **Pictures say who is who.** Every copied graph picture has a legend with each player's colour, you marked.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
