Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.45: crafts on the in-game panel no longer look like rare drops, and the servers opened since launch are in the list.

- **Crafting uses no grade colours.** On the Card and the Ticker, a craft that worked had a blue edge and one that failed a gold-orange edge, which read like Legend and Unique drops (2026-10-26). A craft that worked now looks like any pickup, with a small white ✓ on its tile. One that failed has a red ✕, a faded tile, a red edge and its cost in red. The success bar is grey and red, and the CRAFT label is plain like RUN.
- **Ten more server pairs.** The pairs that opened after launch are now in the list: Tiamat/Indnath, Ishtar/Fafnir, Gauss/Agnita, Lamuatan/Atiel and Nathara/Tassin in Europe; Hithanya/Nemon in NA East; Fregion/Ereshkigal, Meslamtaeda/Beritra, Hithanya/Nemon and Nania/Hadala in Asia. If you play on one, pick it in Options › Server.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
