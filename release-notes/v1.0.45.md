Kinah Ledger 1.0.45: Overdrive, the wild theme.

- **Overdrive, a new theme.** Four looks that leave the dark panel of bars behind, for the Kinah overlay and the DPS meter.
- **Versus.** Your meter is a fighting game: slanted life gauges, a round timer, the boss as your opponent's bar, and FIGHT! and K.O. slams.
- **Manga.** A page from a battle manga at its climax: ink panels and screentone, brush-stroke bars, a DOOM over the leader and a cut-in on every lead change.
- **Dragon.** A Balaur dragon coils round your meter: every bar is its fire, its eye opens on the leader, and a kill makes it roar.
- **Aura.** Power-up auras: every row burns in its class colour, the leader goes gold with lightning, and LIMIT BREAK when you pass your best.
- **Loud moments, calm meter.** The effects play only when something happens (a pull, a new leader, a kill, your best passed, a rare drop), and the meter sits still while you read it. The figures never move, and nothing covers them.
- **Nine themes in Settings.** The Theme chips now sit in two rows of five.
- **Everywhere.** Every new look works in both windows, at any width and size, in Korean and Chinese, and with the PB ghost.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
