Kinah Ledger 1.0.45: layouts that hold on a 100% screen.

- **No cut-off figures in the Fighter look.** On a screen set to 100% scaling, the damage figure's first digit (a "124.56M") could lose its edge in the Fighter meter look. The figure columns are now sized the way the meter draws the text.
- **The idle line fits its window.** The line shown when nothing is happening (the place, the last run, the next timer) was measured a few pixels short on 100% screens and could be clipped. It is now measured the way it is drawn.
- **Full view keeps its columns level when the zone board arrives late.** The two columns balance again whenever one of the cards that move between them changes height, so "Where to farm" opening after the page drew no longer leaves one column far longer than the other.
- **The DPS meter's pop-ups keep their gap.** Two big-hit numbers on a row sat a pixel closer than intended on a 100% screen.
- **The Combat Power line no longer touches the name** in the standard meter looks.
- **The DPS meter settings page fits its window on a 100% screen.** It was three pixels too tall and showed a scroll bar.
- **A short name keeps all its letters in the Neon look.** On a crowded row the Combat Power now goes first, instead of the name losing its last letter to a fraction of a pixel.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
