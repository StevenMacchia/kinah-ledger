Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.19: it starts with Windows, and bound rewards never count as Kinah.

- **Starts with Windows, on by default.** The installed tracker now registers itself to start at sign-in and waits in the tray for the game, so it is already counting when you log in. It is set once with this update; Options › Startup turns it off, and that choice holds.
- **Bound materials and gear show, and never count as Kinah.** An expedition's reward table can't tell a bound Refining Stone from a plain one (the icon is the same), so an Exploration's bound materials since the Nov 4 patch were valued at Market prices. When the chat calls the item bound, the slot is bound: it shows in the run's loot with no value, and the run goes to the site with its bound items listed apart, so the site can show them and leave them out of the money.
- **Four smaller fixes from a review of this week's changes:** a cooking combo's "x2" pop-up is still credited when the race settles a few seconds after it; the run's own reward table right after a Daeva Pass level reward is read, not skipped; a gathering bar's race is scored when its panel goes; and a clear race (one bar full, the other well behind) is scored at once again, only a close one waits for the bars to stop.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
