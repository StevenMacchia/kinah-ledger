Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.55: your profile on kinahledger.com can show each of your characters apart.

- **Uploads say which character.** Every run the tracker shares now says which of your characters played it: its number in the tracker and the name it has in the window. Your signed-in profile on kinahledger.com uses it to show each character's Kinah per hour, today's Kinah, best run and top loot on their own, beside All characters. Only your own profile shows the names, never a public profile or a leaderboard, and they are deleted with your profile. Runs shared before this update stay under All characters.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
