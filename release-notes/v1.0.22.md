Kinah Ledger 1.0.22: meter problems found without a report.

- **The tracker now tells Kinah Ledger, anonymously, whether the DPS meter works on your PC,** so problems get found and fixed without you sending a report. It goes with the reading checks already shared with each run: whether the meter is running or what keeps it off, whether your own hits were recognized and how long that took, and how many fights showed your damage. Counts only, never names or chat.
- **Sharing off means nothing goes.** With sharing off in Settings, none of it leaves your PC, as before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
