Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.35: the new kinahledger.com look, in the app.

- **Near-black with Kinah gold.** The plum and purple are gone. The window, the panel over the game, the item cards and the share pictures now use the website's colours: a near-black background and gold as the one accent, for Kinah and the main buttons. Green and red mean up and down, and items keep the game's grade colours.
- **A new typeface.** Inter replaces the rounded Nunito, as on the site, and text is a little lighter. Cards have smaller corners, and switches and check boxes turn gold when on.
- **The panel over the game is darker.** Its background is a little darker than before, so its figures read at least as well over bright scenes.
- **Night is the new look.** If you picked Abyss or Daylight under Options › General › Theme, you keep it.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
