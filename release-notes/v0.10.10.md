Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.10: the Events page's place cards lose the run timeline and the runs table; what they said is already on the page.

- **A place's card is its figures and its loot.** The card for an event or expedition (Shugo Festival, Urugugu Canyon) showed, under its four figures, a NOW box with the last feed lines and the open run's steps (Entered · In the run · Reward screen · Paid), then a table of the runs behind "Show all 47 runs". ALL RUNS at the top of the page already lists every run with what it gave, and the card's pill already says where the open run stands ("Paid · 1:37 in"), so both are gone. The card is the band, the four figures, LOOT from the runs here, and the Materials, Currencies, Gear and Not named yet groups.
- **A run's sheet still opens from ALL RUNS.** A click on a run there opens its place with the run named ("RUN · Nov 5 18:01 · 2:47") and its sheet under it: the fix for a flagged run, the details and Remove for any other. The sheet has a Close now.
- **A layout render from your own runs.** `KinahLedger.exe ui-shot <dir> --events --mine` draws the Events page from this PC's run log, read from a copy, so a change can be checked against real runs without driving the window. Nothing is tracked, saved or sent.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
