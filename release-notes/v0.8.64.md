Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.64: crafts finished behind the crafting window now count.

- **Crafts behind the crafting window count.** If you opened the crafting window again while a queue was still running, the crafting bars went out of sight and the tracker stopped counting that queue. Two Orichalcum Ingot crafts of 10 counted as one. Now, while the window covers a queue, each pop-up of that recipe's item counts as a craft that worked, with the amount it shows. Nothing else counts as a craft, and a craft that fails behind the window still isn't counted, because it shows nothing.
- **Garbled crafting titles are recognized.** A few misread "Crafting in Progress" titles weren't taken for the crafting panel, which could cost a craft. They are now.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
