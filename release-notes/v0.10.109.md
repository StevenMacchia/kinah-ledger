Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.109: item counting holds through event rewards.

- **No more falling back after a reward.** When an event's reward screen is up, its reward lines reach the chat late. Those late lines no longer read as items the tracker missed, so item counting stays exact through Shugo Festival and other event rewards instead of switching to the screen for a while.
- **Real misses still count.** Lines past the reward's own amounts, or for other items, are judged as before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
