Kinah Ledger 1.0.20: your own fights count from the moment you start the tracker, the fight graph comes first, and the overlays say why when they can't show.

- **Your hits count even if the tracker starts after the game.** Before, a tracker opened after logging in could miss who you are, so your damage, heals, buffs on yourself and kills stayed off the meter. It now finds you much sooner, and at the latest on your next teleport or map change.
- **A line when your own hits aren't showing.** If the meter sees your group fight but none of your hits yet, the Parser page and the Overlays menu say so and tell you what to do, with a one-click report.
- **The fight graph comes first on the Parser page.** One compact header line and a two-line summary; the graph takes the rest of the window and the group table follows.
- **Resizing a window keeps its size.** Dragging an edge no longer jumps the window to the wrong size.
- **Exclusive fullscreen is caught.** When AION 2 runs in exclusive fullscreen (including a game resolution larger than your monitor), the tracker tells you to set Display mode to Borderless so the overlays can show.
- **Report a miscount sends straight away.** No question box pops over the game.
- **Reports say more.** Every report includes where the overlays are and whether they're shown, so a missing overlay can be explained.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
