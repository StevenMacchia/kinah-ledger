Kinah Ledger 1.0.35: the whole party on the meter, graphs where they help, and memory that no longer creeps up.

- **Nobody left off the meter.** A party member who was already in the dungeon before you could be missing from the DPS meter for the whole run. Their damage now counts, and their name comes from the party list on your screen.
- **Graphs on boss fights only.** The Parser page draws its DPS graph for boss fights. Other fights open straight on the group's table.
- **No more slow memory creep.** The tracker held on to a little memory each time it drew text in its own font, close to 20 MB for every hour of play. It now reads the font from a copy on disk, which holds nothing.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
