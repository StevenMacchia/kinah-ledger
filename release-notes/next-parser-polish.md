Kinah Ledger tracker: the Parser gets the main page's look.

- **A Parser that matches the rest of the app.** The Parser tab now uses the same cards, type and bars as the Kinah Ledger page: the boss up top with its clock and its HP on the glowing bar, then your group in its own card. Each member's bar, name and emblem ring share their class colour everywhere they appear.
- **Ability icons.** Click a member to open their abilities: each one has its own icon, a bar in the member's class colour, and its damage, hits, average hit and share.
- **HP left beside the %.** Wherever the boss's HP shows, on the tab and in every overlay look, it now says how much is left too, like "47% · 1.82M left".
- **The meter takes over in dungeons.** In a Transcendence, an Expedition or a Sanctuary, the damage meter replaces the Kinah line for the whole run: before the pull, during it and on the result. A boss or event timer that comes due stays in sight inside the meter. The Kinah line comes back when you leave. Out in the open world nothing changes, and if you split the Parser into its own overlay, the Kinah overlay stays as it is.
- **A wider meter.** Drag the meter's left or right edge to make it wider, the same way you move it. The names and bars stretch, and the figures stay on the right. It's remembered, and there's a Width slider in Settings › In game › Parser too.
- **Group details on by default.** Each member's class, Combat Power and item level show under their name. You can still turn it off in Settings › In game › Parser.
- **Change the overlay's look from the Parser.** The tab shows the look in use ("Overlay look: Auto"). One click opens Settings at the Parser's new Overlay look row, with each look drawn in a boss fight.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
