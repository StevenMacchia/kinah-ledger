Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.12: gathering counted once, rewards kept out of your drops, rare drops protected.

- Gathering counts each gather once. The tracker no longer takes the gathering panel at the bottom of the screen for a pickup, and an item the game upgrades as you gather (Orichalcum Ore turning into Fine Orichalcum Ore) counts as one item, the one you got. A gather you stop or that fails counts nothing.
- Loot beside your character counts only when the chat names it too. If the chat misses a gather, it still counts when the gathering bar finished on Success.
- Rewards from achievements, boxes, chests and quests no longer show up as drops. Expedition and event chests still count for that run.
- An event's rewards that appear a few seconds after the game moves you out now count for that event, even when the chat shows them all at once.
- Legend, Unique and Epic drops and world boss gear always count, also during a world boss fight. Each one is read twice to make sure of its name, and a small picture of the line it came from is kept on this PC (the proof folder beside your logs).
- Farming and gathering at the same time shows one steady set of numbers: Kinah per hour, total per hour, kills per hour and attempts per hour.
- Clear, This session and Earlier are buttons now, easier to find on the Farming and Events pages.
- With sharing on, a few numbers about how well reading went (no chat, no names) go with your shared runs, so future versions can be checked against this one.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
