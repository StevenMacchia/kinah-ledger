Kinah Ledger 1.0.11: meter bars are each player's share, Share on the Kinah overlay, and your party found even when the tracker starts partway into a dungeon.

- **Started or restarted mid-dungeon? Your party still counts.** If the tracker opened (or restarted for an update) after you entered a dungeon, it missed the dungeon and your party, so the meter showed open world and only you. It now picks both up as you play on, and logs the boss fights in the right dungeon and mode.
- **Share from the Kinah overlay.** With the DPS meter inside the Kinah overlay, point at it and a Share button appears under it. A click copies the meter as a picture, even with click-through on; clicks anywhere else still go to the game.
- **Bars show each player's share.** On every meter look, a bar's length is that player's share of the group's damage, so the bars add up to 100%. The top player's bar is no longer always full.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
