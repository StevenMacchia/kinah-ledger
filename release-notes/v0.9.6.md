Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.6: the site's price stands; other servers can't talk it down.

- **A price the site picked is no longer thrown out by other servers.** Kukuru Fried Meat on Siel was 11,800, the lowest of 30 listings, and the tracker valued it at a fraction of that: it compared the price with a "region middle" of its own, built from the other servers' boards, and one lone 1,450 listing on Fregion was enough to call Siel's price a joke ask (2026-11-01). The site already holds every price it publishes against the item's own history and the other servers before it goes out, so the tracker now takes it as given. Only a price the tracker picks itself, from an older board without the site's pick, is still held to the region.
- **A single listing on another server is no longer a region price.** The other servers' middle, which values an item your server's board has no price for, now comes from sure prices (2 or more listings) only. Two lone listings used to make a "sure" middle.
- The "sells for" figures on the crafting Card and in the Crafting part are after the Market's 12% cut, as before: 11,800 shows as 10.4K.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings, goals and history are kept.
