Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.6: the window now tells you whether it is working and whether sharing got through.

- **"Is it working?" has an answer.** Under the zone the window says "Reading · last pickup 4s ago". If nothing has come from the chat for about two minutes while the game is in front, it asks "Nothing read for 2 min. Is chat visible?". The tray icon's hover text says what the tracker is doing: waiting for the game, reading, paused, farming, or update ready.
- **Sharing is described as it works.** What's shared (and the welcome screen) now say that counts go to the site in small batches about every 15 minutes while you play, and that the Share switch turns it off. Before, they said totals go once, when you press Stop.
- **A batch the site doesn't take is no longer lost.** It waits on this PC, up to a day's worth, and goes out with the next one. The ⋯ menu shows "Sharing: last sent 12:04", and the footer says "Sharing: couldn't reach the site, will retry" while a batch is waiting.
- **One error no longer means a dialog every half second.** A problem in the window's refresh is written to the log and the footer says "Something went wrong, see log", at most once every few minutes. The dialog for any other error is plain English with a button to open the log folder, and doesn't repeat for a minute.
- **The setup check fits small screens.** On a screen narrower than the window (a 1080p display at 150% scaling) it scales down instead of running off the edge.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
