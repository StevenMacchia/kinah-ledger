Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.50: what you gather in a dungeon is that dungeon's.

- **Odyle gathered inside a dungeon counts there.** Odyle (or any gathering material) you gather during a dungeon run is now that run's loot, with its kills and drops, instead of landing in the gathering of the zone you return to afterwards. Its gathering attempts stay with the run too, so your open-world gathering pace isn't changed by them. Gathering in an instance zone with no run open counts as gathering, as before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
