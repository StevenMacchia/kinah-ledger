Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.123: a heal chip you can read.

- **The heal chip says what it is.** A healer's row now shows a solid green HEAL tab with their healing a second beside it, at the same size as the name, for example "HEAL 7.8K/s". The figure was a small green number with no label before.
- **TOP HEALS shows the figure too.** After a boss kill, the top healer's chip reads TOP HEALS with their healing a second. Before, the plate could push the figure off the row.
- **The chip fits narrow meters.** In the capsule, the float and the expanded meter, the tab shrinks to a green + and the name trims, so the healing still shows whole. Before, it disappeared there.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
