Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.62: a cleaner, calmer window.

- **Your characters, up top.** The header shows the character the tracker is counting, with its class icon. Click it to see every character on this server: when each was last played, its last 7 days and its best hour, with Rename on each. The tracker still follows whoever you're playing.
- **What matters stands out.** The big card stays as it was; the bag and Next up sit on one surface; Just dropped, Where to farm, Who pays and Drop odds are quieter, so your eye goes where it should.
- **Every number once.** Kinah, loot and kills are now one line inside the top card instead of three boxes under it, and figures that were shown two or three times are shown once. The window is shorter for it.
- **The hour's trend.** A small line beside the big figure shows the last 30 minutes, so you can see at a glance if your hour is going up or down. Click it for the full chart.
- **Full view without the hole.** The two columns now end level instead of leaving an empty block.
- **Colours that mean one thing.** Gold is money and progress, green is a gain or a success, grade colours are items, and links have their own colour.
- **Easier to read.** No running text below 12 px, and the grey text is lighter, on all three looks.
- **The same buttons on every page.** The site and Settings buttons are on every page with tooltips; Share a picture and Full view are in the Farming page's ⋯ menu.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
