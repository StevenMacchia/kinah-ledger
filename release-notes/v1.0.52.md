Kinah Ledger 1.0.52: the whole Buddy all the time, one look for the Buddy and the meter, and a Dragon with teeth

- **The whole Buddy, even before you start counting.** Until the tracker counted something, the Buddy showed only a short line of text. If you have farmed today, it now shows its full card with today's Kinah per hour and today's total, marked "today".
- **The Buddy and the DPS meter share one look.** Pick a look for either one and the other takes it too. On this update, the Buddy switches to the look your DPS meter uses.
- **A fiercer Dragon.** The Dragon look's head is redrawn: bigger, armoured, with a blood-red eye, horns, wide-open jaws full of fangs and fire pouring out. The Buddy, the skills card and the run ring wear the same head, and the body has sharper spikes and a claw gripping the frame.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
