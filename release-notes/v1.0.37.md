Kinah Ledger 1.0.37: see where your damage went between two pulls of the same boss.

- **Pull against pull.** Open a boss pull on the Parser page and pick Compare. It sets the pull beside your best kill of that same boss, in the same place and mode, or beside any other pull of it you pick. You get your DPS and the fight length in both, then every ability side by side: damage, DPS and its change, casts, crit rate (when crit is turned on) and its share of your damage.
- **The biggest losses in plain words.** The three abilities that lost the most DPS are named at the top, for example "Pummel: 55.4% less DPS (−1,536), 6 fewer casts". It says what changed, never a guess at why.
- **Your party too.** Anyone who was in both pulls gets a chip; click it to see their abilities side by side.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
