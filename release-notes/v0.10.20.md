Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.20: hover tips stay inside the window too.

- **Hover tips stay in the window.** The box that shows when you point at a slot, a button or a line (an item's card, what a button does) used to open as a box of its own, which could hang past the tracker's edge and over the game. It now shows inside the tracker's window, kept within its edges, like the click cards in 0.10.19. It stays up while you point at the thing, and goes when you move off it, click, or click back into the game.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
