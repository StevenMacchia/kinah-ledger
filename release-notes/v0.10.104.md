Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.104: Buddy by default, and a meter that grows up.

- **Buddy is the default overlay look.** New installs start on Buddy, and so do players who never picked a look. A look you picked yourself stays.
- **Pick your overlay on first run.** Setup has a new step: hover a look to see it large, click to pick it. You can change it any time in Settings › In game.
- **The Parser's meter grows up.** Its bottom edge stays where you put it. When the meter gets taller (a pull starts, more members join, you expand it), it grows upward and never covers what's below it. Restarting keeps that bottom edge too.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
