Kinah Ledger 1.0.39: meet the companion, a little winged friend who lives through your session with you.

- **A companion over the game, if you want one.** It's off until you turn it on from the Overlays button (or Settings › Companion). It's a small winged wisp that floats beside the Kinah overlay, glances around, smiles, blinks, leans in when a boss fight starts and dozes off after a long quiet. Buddy stays exactly as it is; the companion is an extra.
- **It reacts to what really happens.** A rare drop makes it leap in a burst of sparks in the drop's grade colour. A new best on a boss slams a crown on its head ("NEW BEST · Gadioton +8.2%"). A boss down gets confetti, three big hits in a row set it on fire, a watched boss coming up gets an alert, a takedown gets crossed blades, a wipe gets a rain cloud, and when you fall it sees stars. Every reaction comes from something the tracker already shows; nothing is guessed.
- **Light on your PC.** It's drawn, not a picture, and sits still between its little floats, so it costs about 1% of one CPU core while idle.
- **Yours to tune.** Each reaction has its own switch and a Try button. Lines can be turned off for a silent companion. Drag it anywhere, turn on click-through, and it follows your overlay theme: gold Classic, pearl Modern, a cyan Tactical hologram, a Wacky sticker or a Glitch terminal.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
