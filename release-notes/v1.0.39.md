Kinah Ledger 1.0.39: figures stay whole on 100% screens.

- **No cut-off figures in the Fighter look.** On a screen set to 100% scaling, the damage figure's first digit (a "124.56M") could lose its edge in the Fighter meter look. The figure columns are now sized the way the meter draws the text.
- **The idle line fits its window.** The line shown when nothing is happening (the place, the last run, the next timer) was measured a few pixels short on 100% screens and could be clipped. It is now measured the way it is drawn.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
