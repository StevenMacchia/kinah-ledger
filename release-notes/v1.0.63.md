Kinah Ledger 1.0.63: Kinah per hour follows your kills and your teleports

- **Kinah per hour moves with your kills again.** Most monsters now drop their Kinah as Kina (Bound), in every zone, not only in the Abyss. The tracker set all bound Kinah aside as quest rewards outside the Abyss, so Kinah per hour, the zone's Kinah and each enemy's Kinah stood still while you farmed. A kill's bound Kinah now counts wherever you are.
- **A teleport moves the zone figures at once.** A teleport in the open world shows no loading screen, so the "here" figures used to wait a minute on the old zone. Now the teleport's fee followed by a new zone on the minimap is a move right away.
- **Quest rewards stay out.** Bound Kinah with no kill of yours beside it, or more than a kill pays, is still left out as a reward.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
