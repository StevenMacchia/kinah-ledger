Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.45: Poko's Skin Bundle counts on Gathering.

- **Poko's Skin Bundle is a rare find.** Any gathering node can give one, and the tracker used to miss it twice over: it took the line for a box being opened and didn't know the name. Now it counts on the Gathering page under its own "Rare finds" group, at the top, and in the bag ahead of everything else.
- **Rare though it has no price.** It's bound, so the Market has no price for it, but it's treated like a rare drop: the overlay shows it, the chime plays if you have it on, and it glows in the game's teal when it lands. It doesn't count as a node gathered, so your gathering pace stays the same.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
