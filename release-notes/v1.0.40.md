Kinah Ledger 1.0.40: race your best kill, live, like a racing game's ghost.

- **PB ghost.** On a boss you've killed before (the same boss, place and mode), the DPS meter races your best kill of it. A slim line on your bar shows where your best's pace would put you. The gap to it lights up green when you're ahead and red when you're behind, and a small chip beside it says "+6.2%" or "−3.1%". Point at the chip for the full words.
- **Fair to the second.** Your pull and your best are lined up by the boss's HP, so you're compared at the same point of the fight. At the kill the figure lands exactly on your final DPS difference.
- **At the kill.** A gold "PB +4.1%" with a quick glint, or "−2.3%". It's the same figure as the PB chip.
- **Light on the meter.** It adds no height and moves smoothly, and it hides its chip when a row is too tight, leaving the line. It works in every meter look and theme.
- **Your group too, if you like.** Settings › DPS meter › More › PB ghost has a Group pace switch that adds the group's pace to the chip. PB ghost is on by default, and you can turn it off there.
- **Your bests from before count.** If the fight that set your best is still among your kept fights, its ghost is ready the first time you pull that boss again.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
