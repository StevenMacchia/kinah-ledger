- **Your boss bests on your profile, if you want them.** A new switch in Options › Sharing, **Show my boss bests on my kinahledger.com profile**, off unless you turn it on. On, your own best kill on each boss (your DPS, the fight's length and date) and your latest pull of it show on your signed-in profile at kinahledger.com, per character, for you alone. Only your own numbers go: your group's names and DPS stay on your PC, and nothing is ranked. Turn it off and the site forgets them.
