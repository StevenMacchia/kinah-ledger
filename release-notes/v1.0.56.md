Kinah Ledger 1.0.56: every Buddy says more, and the figures are more exact and quicker

- **Every Buddy look says what the Classic card does.** Under the hour, each look now shows how long you have been counting, how far you are from your best here, and your newest pickup with its worth, in the look's own style.
- **A truer Kinah an hour.** Kills, and the bound Kinah they pay, now count as farming time, so a fast-killing stretch no longer reads far above what it really made. The time between zones is kept by the zone you left, and the "here" hour counts farming time as the main figure does.
- **Drops show up at once.** A monster's drop is counted about half a second after it lands, not 3 to 5 seconds later, and the Buddy redraws on every count instead of on the next half-second tick.
- **No price for joke listings.** A lone listing far above anything the item sells for no longer counts, even on items no other server lists.
- **Gear you dismantle right away isn't loot.** A piece broken into Enhance Stones right after it dropped no longer counts at its market price.
- **The Abyss hour over your farming time there,** and steadier while you warm up.
- **Honest boss records.** A pull where part of the boss's HP or part of the group's damage went unseen is marked partial, and a kill seen only in part can't become a record.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
