Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.6: crafts counted from the crafting panel, live.

- Each craft is counted the moment its Success or Fail bar finishes on the crafting panel, under the recipe the panel shows, even when a queue of crafts runs with the chat hidden.
- A combo upgrade roll on the blue panel counts as the upgrade of the craft before it ("combo upgraded n of m" on the Crafting page).
- While you craft, and for 15 seconds after, what the loot list shows is what your crafts made, never a drop from kills.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
