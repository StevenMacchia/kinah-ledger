Kinah Ledger 1.0.61: Killed by and Combat Power on the PvP panel

- **Killed by.** When a player kills you in the Abyss or an arena, the PvP panel adds a row below your kills: Killed by and their name, with a cross where the rank goes and a violet bar that sets it apart from your kills. It shows the damage you landed on them, and hovering it shows your skills on them. It stays until your next kill or assist.
- **Their Combat Power.** Each player you kill or help kill, and the player who killed you, shows their Combat Power beside the name, as it was when they came into view. Where everyone wears the same set gear, or theirs is not known, it shows nothing rather than a wrong figure.
- **Always in view.** In the compact meter the Killed by row takes the place of your oldest kill, so it is never cut off.
- Works in every meter look, in English, Korean and Traditional Chinese.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
