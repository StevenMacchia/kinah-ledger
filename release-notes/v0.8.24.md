Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.24: dungeon Kinah read right, crafting only from the crafting bars.

- Expedition reward tables are read whole: every slot and count, the coin included, even when the table closes a second after it opens. The 600.0K coin counts as Kinah, at the exact amount your Kinah counter rose by.
- A run whose coin or table couldn't be read says "Kinah not read" and is left out of the runs, per hour, per run and total, instead of counting as 0.
- Crafting counts only crafts whose Success or Fail bar you saw finish, and what each makes. Drops, loot list rows and chat lines never land in Crafting, and the loot list isn't read while the crafting window is up.
- Pickups while you craft go where they belong, never under Crafting.
- A screen that only looks like a reward table can no longer start or split a run.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
