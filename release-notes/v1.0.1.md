Kinah Ledger 1.0.1: the Buddy stays on screen, and Options is easier to get around.

- **The Buddy no longer disappears mid-game.** Where it sat over a part of the game the tracker reads, it blinked out on every read. It stays put now, and so does the DPS meter.
- **Buddy and DPS meter pages in Options.** Each overlay has its own page on the left bar.
- **Pick the DPS meter's look on its own.** The DPS meter page has its own Classic, Wacky, Tactical and Glitch chips, so the meter's look no longer depends on the Buddy's theme.
- **Save keeps Options open.** It says Saved., and Close or Cancel leaves.
- **Every look's preview is whole.** Fighter and Holo were cut off in Options.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
