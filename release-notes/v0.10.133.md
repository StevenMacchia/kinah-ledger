Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.133: see what each specialty does, and preview overlay looks over the game.

- **Clickable specialties.** In the Parser's ability table, the gold "SPEC 4·5" tag after a skill's name is now a button. Click it to open a card with that skill's five specializations. The ones you picked are lit, each with what it does and the skill level it needs. Hover over the tag for a quick list. The game itself calls them only "Specialization 1" to "5", so the card shows those numbers with each effect. The effects come from MetaBot's skill pages and cover 200 skills; passives and stances have no specializations.
- **Look previews over the game.** In Settings › In game, the overlay look thumbnails and the Parser's look preview now sit on a real game scene instead of a dark box, so you can tell how each look reads in play.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
