Kinah Ledger 1.0.64: PvP damage is back, and a loading screen moves the zone at once

- **PvP damage is back on the kills panel.** Since the Nov 25 patch the panel showed each player's Combat Power but 0 damage, and assists didn't show at all. Your damage on each player you kill, help kill or die to is back, now with your damage-over-time ticks too.
- **Through a loading screen, the zone figures switch at once.** Going into the Abyss (or anywhere behind a loading screen) kept the "here" figures and Abyss Points per hour on the old zone for about a minute, because the loading screen itself was never noticed. The zone read right after it now moves them straight away.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
