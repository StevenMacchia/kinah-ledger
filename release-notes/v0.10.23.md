Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.23: the window in the Quest look.

- **A new look for the whole window.** Deep plum, Kinah gold and candy accents, a heavier rounded face, and an icon for every kind of thing. Every page keeps three things above the fold and puts the rest behind a hover or a click, so it stays glanceable while you farm and goes deep when you want it to. Night is the Quest look; Abyss is a navy of its own; Daylight keeps its white cards on a light page.
- **Farming: the chase.** The hour is still the big figure, and under it sits the one line that keeps you farming: how far you are from your best here, as a bar that fills toward a flag and as a ring you can read from across the room. Beat it and the ring turns mint with a star. The rate chart opens on a click.
- **Three chips, not a wall.** Kinah, loot and kills under the hero; each opens on a click: Kinah by enemy, the bag, kills by enemy. The bag's slots with their counts sit under the chips, so Compact shows what you have got this session at a glance.
- **Next up.** Two milestones worked out from your pace, each a few minutes away: the round Kinah figure the session is about to pass, and the stack of a thing you are collecting. They turn over by themselves.
- **Just dropped, with the game's icons.** The last five pickups, each with the item's own icon and what it was worth in mint; enemy and time in the small print. Hover for the facts, click for the big card.
- **Full view.** The bag as tiles with the game's icons, Kinah by enemy as bars, drop odds as "1 in 4" badges, the zone board as bars with a flag on the zone you are in.
- **Crafting.** Profit is the hero, in mint (coral at a loss), with the ring of crafts that worked. Next up names the next round profit and the recipe that gets you there, then the recipe that pays most today. Each craft shows its item with a mint check or a coral cross; the recipes this session are small cards.
- **Expeditions and events.** The open run is a timer: the ring and the bar count it against the average run here, so "almost paid" reads without a number. Three chips say what the counted runs paid out. Runs is the last three on their lines, with every run behind "earlier runs"; Paid out here is the rewards as slots with their counts. A run with something to look at gets its own card.
- **The panel over the game: Badge, Buddy and Pop.** The three looks keep your choice and take the new look. Badge is the strip with a small ring of your best here; Buddy is the card with the ring beside the hour and your best under the session total; Pop is the ticker with loot popping mint and Kinah gold. The ring is the same meter everywhere.
- **No ovals.** Every button and switch is a rounded rectangle; bars, the chart and the ring glow a little in their colour; cards stand off the page in every look.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
