Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.85: the main page as it was, Inventory in its own window, your gear under your name.

- **The main page is back to how it was.** The Inventory card and the gear row are off the Farming page.
- **Inventory has its own window.** Open it from the ⋯ menu on the Farming page. It opens on what dropped this session and what it sells for at today's Market prices, after the Market's cut. Bound items can't be sold, so they have no value there. It sits beside the game, moves where you drag it, and remembers where you put it.
- **The whole bag only if you pick it.** "The bag" shows everything in your character's bag, including what you had before you started (never your storage). It never opens there by itself.
- **Your gear under your name.** Click your character's name at the top to see the gear you wear, each piece with its +level. A piece the item list doesn't know yet shows as a plain slot with its level until its picture is added.
- **More detail if you want it.** Options › Game reading › Inventory details adds what is bound, what has no price yet, the change since you started, and each item's price on hover.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
