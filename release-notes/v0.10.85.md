Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.85: a cleaner window, your timers one click away, and a quiet timer overlay.

- **Pages across the top.** Farm, Craft, Runs and Timers sit in the header. The Timers tab shows the next countdown you watch when it is under 15 minutes. Quests opens from Next up on the Farm page.
- **The Farm page is back to how it was.** The inventory card and the gear row are gone from it.
- **Timers page.** The event you are in, then the timers you watch, then every map's other world bosses, folded. A bell on each row adds it to the overlay and its chime. Pop out opens the full list in its own window for a second screen.
- **One quiet timer overlay.** A narrow strip over the game for events and world bosses: hidden until something you watch is 5 minutes away, then a soft chime. Clicks pass through it; hold Ctrl to move it or right-click it for its menu. Watched by default: the four events, Soul Ruler Kashapa, Eternal Gartua, High Commander Lagta and every Abyss boss.
- **Abyss world bosses.** Lower and Middle Reshanta bosses are on the Timers page, on your region's time.
- **Times in your region's time.** NA East events and bosses follow US Eastern time with daylight saving. The Spacetime Rift is every 3 hours from 02:00, and the Artifact Siege and its three Executors are Thursday and Saturday. Other regions use their local time until checked, marked in orange.
- **One Overlays button.** The round button in the header switches the Kinah and Timers overlays; a green dot shows when one is on. The site link moved to the bottom, beside Discord.
- **Inventory has its own window,** from the ⋯ menu on the Farm page: what dropped this session and what it sells for after the Market's cut. Bound items have no value. "The bag" shows your character's bag (never storage) only when you pick it.
- **Your gear under your name.** Click your character's name at the top to see the gear you wear, each piece with its +level.
- **Minimal by default.** More detail for the inventory and the timers is a switch in Options.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
