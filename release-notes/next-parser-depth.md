Kinah Ledger tracker: the Parser keeps the detail behind each fight.

- **More kept for every boss pull.** From now on each pull also keeps, on your PC:
  - every member's damage second by second, and the boss's HP over the fight;
  - each ability's casts and its biggest and smallest hit;
  - the damage each member took, by skill and attacker;
  - each death: when it happened, who killed them and with what, the boss's HP at that moment, and the last hits they took;
  - how long each effect was on each member.

  The Parser's coming views show these. Fights saved before this update stay as they were.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
