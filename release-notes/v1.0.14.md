Kinah Ledger 1.0.14: live fight graphs, everyone asking to join your party at once, and copy buttons you can see.

- **Graphs on the Parser page.** The first tab, live as the fight goes and kept for every pull: each player's DPS over the fight with deaths marked, the boss's HP over the group's stacked damage (when it crossed ⅔ and ⅓, the kill or the wipe), and healing against damage taken. Hover for every player's numbers at that second; each graph has its own Copy picture.
- **Applicants window.** While you lead a party listing, everyone asking to join shows in one list, not one at a time: class, name, server, level, Combat Power and how long ago they asked. They leave the list as they join or when the listing closes. It takes your overlay theme's look; switch it off in Settings › Applicants.
- **Copy buttons you can see.** On the Parser page, the summary, each table and each graph have their own Copy picture and Copy text buttons. Right-click still works too.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
