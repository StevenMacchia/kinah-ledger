Kinah Ledger 1.0.49: a smoother meter on long boss pulls

- **A smoother meter on long boss pulls.** Since 1.0.38 the meter copied your Timeline data on every redraw, and that copy grew with the pull, up to about 14 ms per redraw by 10 minutes in. The meter now leaves that data out while the pull is under way. The Timeline tab still has it all once the pull is kept.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
