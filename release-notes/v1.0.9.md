Kinah Ledger 1.0.9: Uptime at the foot of the DPS meter.

- **Uptime instead of Peak.** When you fight alone, the meter's foot used to show your best 5 seconds, which was nearly always your opener. It now shows your uptime: the share of the fight you were dealing damage, so time spent running, dead or out of range shows. Your best on a boss you have killed before still comes first, and a group still sees the group's DPS.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
