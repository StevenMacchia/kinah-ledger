Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.69: crafts made with bound materials keep their value.

- **What you craft from bound materials isn't bound.** Since 0.10.59, once you had picked up bound copies of a material (Refining Stone from an event, say), everything crafted from it was marked "bound" and valued at nothing. The game doesn't do that: an Orichalcum Guard made with bound Refining Stones comes out tradeable. What a craft makes is now valued at the Market's price again, and the bound materials still cost nothing, since you can't sell them.
- **A misread queue size no longer adds a craft.** One read of a queue of 15 as "45" used to restart the queue's count, and the next craft then counted a craft "not seen" that never happened. A size one digit off the one already read is taken for a misread.
- **A serum's or a dish's combo isn't another craft.** The roll after a craft that won a combo (the blue panel) was sometimes counted as a craft of its own when the recipe's name was read during it. It now counts as the combo.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
