Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.57: the overlay moves like the game's own pop-ups.

- **Calmer, game-like motion.** New pickups slide a short way in from the right and fade in quickly, as the game's own pickup pop-ups do. Pops are smaller, and glows and gold edges are softer and shorter, on Pop, Buddy and Badge.
- **Digit flip.** When your hour or session figure changes on a pickup, only the digits that changed flip up into place, right to left.
- **Rare drop sparks.** A rare drop's tile sends out one small burst of sparks in its grade's colour as it lands.
- **Hot streak bar.** A thin gold bar on the overlay fills as pickups come quickly and drains when you slow down.

Each plays once when something changes. With Windows's animations turned off, none of it plays.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
