Kinah Ledger 1.0.65: the Parser page keeps up in boss fights

- **The Parser page no longer lags in a boss fight.** With the page open during a pull (the graphs and the group under them), the page fell behind the fight: a hit took about a tenth of a second to show, and up to a second at worst. The group table was being built twice for every hit, and the whole page was laid out again up to 16 times a second. The table is now built once, the list of kept pulls stays as it is until a pull ends, and the page redraws four times a second while the overlay still keeps up with every hit. In a test pull with eight players, a hit now shows in about a third of the time it took before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
