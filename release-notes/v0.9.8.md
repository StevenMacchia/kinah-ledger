Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.8: Abyss Points show as a slot beside Kinah in the Abyss.

- **Abyss Points are counted where you farm them.** In the Abyss's zones (Reshanta and its gates) the points you pick up show as a slot next to the Kinah, on LOOT's This zone side and on Today, with the amount and an hour's pace on hover. Elsewhere, from quests or events, they are not counted as farming.
- **Kept through restarts.** Today's total and each zone's figure carry over when the tracker is closed and opened again.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
