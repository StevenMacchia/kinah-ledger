Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.57: signed, so Windows knows who made it.

- **Signed installer and app.** The installer, the tracker and every file it installs now carry a digital signature from Steven Macchia, checked by Microsoft. Windows shows him as the publisher instead of "unknown publisher", and right-clicking a file and opening Properties › Digital Signatures shows the signature.
- Windows' "protected your PC" notice can still show for a while on a fresh download. SmartScreen trusts a new signed publisher gradually as more people install, so it fades without anything else changing.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
