Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.128: healing that counts, solid bars, short numbers, boss HP thirds.

- **Healing a second leaves out overheal.** The green figure on a healer's row now counts only healing that actually healed someone. On a Cleric, 50 to 85% of all healing was overheal, which made the old figure far too big. Hover over it for the full picture: total healed, the part that landed, and the overheal %. TOP HEALS and heal records go by this figure too.
- **Chanter mantras count for the Chanter.** Heals from Sprint Mantra and other Chanter skills came in under each player they landed on, as that player's own self-healing. They now count toward the Chanter. If the group has two Chanters, nothing is moved, because there's no telling which one cast it.
- **Heal bars show each healer's share** of the fight's healing, over a faint track the width of the row.
- **Solid damage bars.** The class-colored bars behind each row are much more opaque, so they're easy to see.
- **Short numbers.** The meter writes 32.5K instead of 32,512: DPS, group DPS, the total and the heal figures. Tooltips and the Parser page tables keep the full numbers.
- **Boss HP thirds.** Every boss HP bar has a small notch at ⅓ and ⅔.

Your heal records start over in a new file, measured by healing that landed. They're rebuilt from the fights you already have. Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
