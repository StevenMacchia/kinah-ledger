Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.43: a calmer Ticker that keeps your newest pickups and the session's top drop.

- **No "to look at" line on the panel.** The Ticker showed an orange "23 to look at" under an idle pill (2026-10-26), which read like an error. The in-game panel no longer shows the accuracy line in any look; the tracker window still lists what to look at. Turning on Options › Show accuracy details puts it back on the panel.
- **The Ticker keeps the three newest pickups and your top drop.** Pickups stay on the Ticker until a newer one pushes the oldest out, instead of fading after 6 seconds, so a quiet moment no longer leaves it empty. The fourth slot is the session's most valuable item, marked TOP DROP. A notice, such as the reader pausing, takes the oldest pickup's place.
- **No "0:00 | 0" before anything is counted.** Until the first pickup, the Ticker's pill says "Waiting for the first count".

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
