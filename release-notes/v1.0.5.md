Kinah Ledger 1.0.5: the app's Wacky, Tactical and Glitch skins wear their themes.

- **Skins with the themes' art.** The Wacky, Tactical and Glitch app skins now draw their overlay themes' look through the whole window: the hero, frames, headings, rows, bars and the window's ground, and the new Options pages too.
- **Parser page on a narrow window.** The "Only boss fights / Always on" pick no longer slides under the Overlay switch; it wraps to the next line.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
