Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.67: a local recording of the game's traffic, for tracking down miscounts.

- **The tracker can keep a recording of its game traffic, for diagnosis.** Off for everyone unless switched on (`KinahLedger.exe packet-log on`). While on, the running tracker keeps a copy of the game server's traffic it already listens to, one file an hour in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\KinahLedger\packets`, kept for 48 hours and 2 GB at most. It stays on your PC: nothing is uploaded. `packet-log off` stops it.
- **`packet-record` records a few minutes on demand**, with the tracker's chat lines from the same minutes, without an administrator terminal. Ctrl+C stops it early and still saves.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
