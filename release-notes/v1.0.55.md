Kinah Ledger 1.0.55: an honest Abyss Points hour

- **Abyss Points an hour counts your time in the Abyss.** It used to divide your points by the whole session, so time spent farming elsewhere first dragged the figure down (285 points in about a minute there read 605 an hour). The Abyss Points and Rank Points hours on the Buddy and the Farming page now count only your time in the Abyss, and show "warming up" for your first 3 minutes there.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
