Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.125: Korean and Traditional Chinese.

- **한국어 and 繁體中文.** The DPS meter, the Parser page, Settings and the first-run screens can now show Korean or Traditional Chinese. By default the tracker follows Windows' language, so a Korean or Taiwanese Windows gets its own language. Pick one in Settings › General › Language; the change applies the next time Kinah Ledger starts. Dates follow the language too.
- **Still in English for now:** the rest of the main window (Farming, Crafting, Events, Quests), overlay look and theme names, and skill, boss, item and server names, which come from the game's English data. The release notes stay in English too.
- The tracker still supports the global servers only (NA, Europe, Latin America, Asia).

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
