Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.34: no accuracy scores, and gathering counted right after using storage.

- **No accuracy figures.** The Farming page, the panel over the game and the Discord card no longer show an accuracy percentage or counts. When the game's own numbers disagree with something the tracker counted, you see "1 to look at", and the item opens with Report and Fine. To see the full details, turn on Options › Game reading › Show accuracy details.
- **Gathering right after storage.** A gather straight after you'd used storage or the bag could be taken for an inventory move and left out; the gathering panel's own result now always counts.
- **No phantom x1.** When a chat line was read cut short ("Acquired Kukuru" with its count missing), it could be counted again as a new x1. It's now matched to the line it came from, and a count is corrected only when it's read clearly twice.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
