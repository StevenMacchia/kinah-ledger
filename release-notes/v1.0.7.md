Kinah Ledger 1.0.7: new meter looks, a Modern theme, and runs filed where they belong.

- **A new Modern theme.** Four light, clean looks for the DPS meter, the Buddy and the skills card: **Glass** (frosted panel), **Mono** (black and white), **Line** (a hairline frame with slim bars) and **Fade** (no panel, each row a soft wash of class colour). Pick them in Settings, under the overlays.
- **Reworked looks.**
  - **Forge** is the new standard meter look; **Podium** and **Reactor** sit beside it.
  - **Arena** replaces the Scorebug, with **Studio** next to it.
  - **Viewfinder** replaces VHS and stays readable over the tape; **Deck** is new.
  - **Holo card** takes Goo's place in the Wacky theme.
- **Nothing cut off.** Every meter look keeps every figure whole, even in a full party with long names, big DPS and heals. The bar comes first in each row.
- **Transcendence is logged as Transcendence.** Deus Research Base and the other Transcendence runs were filed as Expeditions; they now land under Transcendence with their stage. A repeat run of the same dungeon gets its bosses' names again instead of "Unnamed boss". Your past runs are fixed once at the next start, and a backup of the old files is kept. Runs already sent to the website stay as they were.
- **Personal bests can be beaten again.** Fight records were keyed to something that changed every run; they now follow the boss.
- **No more "Waiting for the first count".** Before the first count, the overlay shows something useful: your dungeon and last run, today's Abyss Points, or what you made here today, plus the next boss timer when there is room.
- **Parser page.** Every view can be shared as a picture or copied as text, including the timeline. Full run and Every fight are gone; there is one clear view. All boss pulls has a big Back button.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
