Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.49: fewer drops lost when you kill fast, and other players' chat no longer hides your drops.

- **A drop the chat and the loot list both showed counts.** When you kill fast, the chat fades in garbled and scrolls on quickly. A drop line could be read clearly once but be taken for an old line, then scroll away, while the loot list beside your character showed the same item waiting for it (2026-10-27: a Raw Leather lost this way). When a clear chat line that wasn't counted and a loot list row agree on the item and the count, it now counts once. Gathering is unchanged: the gathering bar is already its second check.
- **Other players' chat is never your storage.** A player's message that wrapped onto a second line could mention storage or a purchase, and the tracker took it for your own storage move and set aside your next drop. Lines of other players' messages are now ignored for this.
- **Soul Crystals right after an event count.** The Soul Crystals from your first kills after an event's reward screen were filed as the event's reward. They now count with your kills, like drops already did in 0.8.47.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
