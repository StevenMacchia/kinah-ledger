Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.81: what your inventory is worth.

- **An Inventory card on the Farming page.** It shows what everything your character holds is worth at today's Market prices: the total, the part you could sell after the Market's cut, and how many items have no price yet. Your most valuable items are shown as slots.
- **How much it changed since you started.** The card says how your inventory's worth moved since the tracker first saw it, item by item on hover.
- **Exact, from the game itself.** The game sends your whole inventory when you log in or change server (a teleport to another server, entering or leaving the Abyss), and every change after that. Until it does, the card says so. The gear you wear is left out of the worth. Bound items count at the price of their tradable version and are never counted as sellable.
- **Your gear and its enhancement levels.** The card shows the gear you wear with its +level, as the game sent it.
- **Pickups of items you already carried count from the first one.** The first pickup of an item after you log in used to wait for the chat line. Now the tracker already knows how many you had.
- **Reward lists with gear in them count in full.** A reward that came with gear used to lose the items listed after the first piece of gear.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
