Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.81: world boss timers.

- **World boss timers.** Open the world map in the game once, and the tracker gets every field boss's timer from it. Open them from the ⋯ menu on the Events page, **World boss timers**. Each boss shows with its place and a countdown to its spawn, or "Up now" while the map shows it alive. The window can be dragged next to the game and stays open while you play.
- **The countdowns keep going after you close the map.** They follow the game's own clock, so they match the map to the second. The window says how long ago the map was last open. A boss whose time has passed shows as "Due", and a boss that was up shows as "Was up", until you open the map again.
- **They survive a restart.** The last reading is saved, so the timers are there when the tracker starts, with their age.
- They need packet reading (Options › Game reading), like the counts from the game's traffic.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
