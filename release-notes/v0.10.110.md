Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.110: DPS from your first hit, and no penalty for stopping.

- **DPS from the first hit.** The meter shows a figure the moment your first hit lands, instead of "–" for the first 3 seconds. A hit is never counted over less than a second, so one big hit doesn't read as millions.
- **No penalty for stopping.** In the open world, when you stop attacking, the meter keeps falling for a few seconds. If nothing else is hit, it goes back to the fight's DPS as it stood at your last hit and holds it. Your next hit starts a new fight from 0.
- **Hits taken don't drag it down.** A hit you take after your last one keeps the fight open, but the held DPS is still at your last hit dealt.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
