Kinah Ledger tracker 0.7.5. Trackers on 0.7.2 and later update by themselves; otherwise download KinahLedger.exe and run it.

- Loot tables for every enemy MetaBot.GG lists (1,139 with drops). Every enemy under Kinah by enemy opens to its drop pool, the drop card lists the enemies in your zone that drop the item, and odds count only kills of enemies that can drop it.
- Skins count: 1,626 skins from MetaBot.GG. The chat's "Dawn Frost (Cloak) Skin" is the skin "Dawn Frost (Skin: Cloak)".
- An empty section in the per-zone view says what came in other zones, so loot never looks lost after a zone change.

Settings, history and logs are kept.
