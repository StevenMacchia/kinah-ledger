Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.100: the Kinah overlay and the Parser are two windows again.

- **Two windows.** The Kinah overlay stays the Kinah overlay, in the open world and in dungeons, and the Parser has its own window you can drag anywhere. With the Parser's meter Always on, fighting in the open world no longer turns the Kinah overlay into the Parser.
- **For everyone.** Every installed copy moves to the two windows, whatever was set before. To have the Parser inside the Kinah overlay again, turn on Settings › In game › Parser › Parser inside the Kinah overlay.
- **The header's Overlays menu** has a switch for each: Kinah and Parser.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
