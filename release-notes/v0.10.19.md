Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.19: cards open inside the window, never over the game.

- **Cards stay in the window.** A click on an item, a menu (⋯, the sort and place dropdowns), a goal, a report or the run recap used to open its card as a box of its own, which could hang past the tracker's edge and over the game. Every one of them now opens inside the tracker's window and is kept within its edges; one taller than the window scrolls inside it. A click outside the card, Esc, or clicking back into the game closes it, as before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
