Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.89: timers on the Kinah overlay, overlays you just drag, the latest run first.

- **Timers on the Kinah overlay.** When something you watch is a few minutes away, a small line appears on top of the Kinah overlay ("Taulo 3:59 · Shugo 5:30"), with a soft chime. The Kinah line never moves; the timers sit above it, and above the Parser during a boss fight. Settings › Timers › Separate timer overlay gives them their own strip instead.
- **Every overlay moves by dragging it.** The separate timer strip and the separate Parser overlay move like the Kinah overlay: click and drag them anywhere, and they stay there. They look like the Kinah overlay too. Click-through is an option, off by default; when it is on, a small grip appears on hover to drag by. Reset position is in Settings.
- **Runs open on your latest run.** The Runs page shows the run you just did first (All runs is one click away), then what this place has paid out, then Best drops, folded until you open it and sortable by value, count, name or when. The list of runs is one short line each.
- **Crafting opens only once a craft is done.** Opening the crafting window or browsing recipes no longer switches the tracker to Crafting; it switches when a craft completes, and goes straight back to farming at your next kill or gather.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
