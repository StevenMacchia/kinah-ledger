Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.22: remove a run the tracker got wrong.

- In Expeditions and events, open a place and click a run that's wrong. After you confirm, it leaves the list and is never sent to the site. A copy is kept aside, so nothing is lost for good.
- Use it for the runs that were saved as one before 0.8.20 (back-to-back expedition runs merged together).

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
