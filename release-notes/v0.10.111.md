Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.111: double harvest procs count in full.

- **Plentiful and Bountiful Harvest together.** When both procs land on the same gather, every resource they give is counted, with its right amount. Before, the first gather of an item you didn't have yet could count only the bigger of the two.
- **No double counting either.** A line that belongs to your next gather is never added to the one before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
