Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.134: overlay themes. Pick Wacky, Tactical or Glitch for nine new looks, each with its own meter and its own moments.

- **Themes for the overlay.** Settings › In game has a new Theme row over the look thumbnails: Classic, Wacky, Tactical and Glitch. Pick a theme to see its looks, then pick a look. Classic keeps every look you had, unchanged; the Capsule now sits at the far right of it.
- **Wacky.** Sticker: comic stickers slapped over the game, the meter a sticker sheet with ink-stamp ranks and tape bars, a POW! on every pickup and K.O.! on a kill. Neon: an arcade cabinet, the meter a high-score table (1ST, 2ND, 3RD) on neon tubes, HIGH SCORE when you pass your best. Goo: a glossy candy jelly, the meter jelly beans with candy bars, pickups bubbling out and popping.
- **Tactical.** Fighter: a jet's HUD in green lines straight over the game, a reticle for your best, the meter a target list, a lock-on on every pickup. Scorebug: a tournament broadcast, the meter a leaderboard with gold, silver and bronze tabs, VICTORY or DEFEAT at the end. Holo: projected cyan glass, the meter a holographic list, a scan line sweeping each pickup.
- **Glitch.** VHS: a camcorder's viewfinder with REC and the tape counter, the meter an on-screen menu list, a tracking band on each pickup. Corrupted: a broken signal, the meter torn data rows, TARGET.DELETED on a kill. Terminal: an old green screen, the meter a `top` table with block bars, each pickup printed as a new line.
- **Every figure, every look.** Each new look shows your Kinah an hour, the session, your best here, the timers due (green ENTER while an entry is open), the Abyss view, and in a fight the boss, its HP with the thirds marked, every member's DPS, share, heals, deaths, Combat Power and TOP DPS / TOP HEALS, with the skills card on hover. The figures never move; the effects play around them, and only while Windows's animations are on.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
