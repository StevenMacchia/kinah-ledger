Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.132: combat power on the meter, your party right away, a fresh meter for every dungeon fight, and a resizable window.

- **Combat power next to each name.** Once damage is coming in, each row shows the player's combat power in short form (150.8K) right after their name. Hover over it for the full number. If a row is tight, it's the first thing to make room.
- **Your party shows up right away.** As soon as people join your group, the meter lists them with their combat power, even before anyone attacks. In a fight, someone who hasn't dealt damage stays on the meter for 15 seconds, then hides until they do.
- **Every fight in a dungeon starts fresh.** Like in the open world, the always-on meter in a dungeon now starts over after a few seconds without fighting. Each trash pack gets its own meter, and boss pulls are still their own.
- **Resize the window by dragging.** Drag any edge or corner of the main window to make it bigger or smaller (80% to 150%). Everything scales together, so nothing gets cut off or scrolls. The size is kept when you let go, the same as Settings › General › Window size or Ctrl+= and Ctrl+-.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
