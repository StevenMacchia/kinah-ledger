Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.73: a boss-fight Parser.

- **A new Parser tab.** It tracks boss fights in Transcendence, Expeditions and Sanctuaries, and nothing else: trash, elites and the open world never make a fight. Each boss is its own fight, and pulling the next boss starts a fresh one.
- **The fight under way, live.** You get the boss, the instance and its stage, a running clock, and your group with each member's class, Combat Power and item level. Damage and DPS show when the game provides them, and are never estimated.
- **Every fight is kept on this PC, per character.** Filter past fights by Transcendence, Expedition or Sanctuary, or by boss to see your record and best kill time. Open a fight to see its group, deaths, timings and the kills near you. Fights are never uploaded.
- **Robust by design.** A lost connection, a reconnect or a tracker restart in the middle of a fight doesn't split it. The fight carries on and is marked partial, with the reason.
- **A fight overlay over the game.** It shows the boss, the clock and the group during boss fights, then the result for a few seconds after. It never takes the game's focus. Drag it anywhere and it stays there, or make it click-through. Turn it on or off on the Parser tab or with Ctrl+Shift+P.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
