Kinah Ledger 1.0.36: share a boss pull in one click.

- **Share this pull.** Every finished boss pull in the Parser page's list has a small picture icon. One click copies a picture of that pull, ready to paste into Discord: the boss, the instance and mode, the pull's time, and the group's DPS rows in your own meter look. It is also saved in Pictures\Kinah Ledger. The pull doesn't open, and nothing is uploaded.
- **Your records show.** When a pull beat your best on that fight, the picture carries a gold "New personal best" badge with how far you beat it.
- **Only your name on a shared picture.** Share pictures now name only you. Everyone else in the group shows as their class, such as "Chanter", with their figures. To show their names too, turn on Settings › In game › Parser › Show party names.
- **When it happened.** Share pictures now give the time the pull started next to the day.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
