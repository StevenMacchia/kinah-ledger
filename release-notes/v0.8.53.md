Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.53: Kinah you get in a base is never farming.

- **Rewards in a base stay out of farming.** Kinah that comes in while you're in a base (a Daeva Pass reward, a sale, the mail) no longer counts as farming Kinah or as a kill. Your Farming numbers and the Today line only show what you farmed.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
