Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.15: crafting that tells you the truth, prices that say how old they are, and a tidier window.

**Crafting**

- **The recipe card leads with its verdict.** Profit per craft sits under the recipe name, green or red, with profit an hour once you have crafted it three times and the break-even sell price under it. The materials and the rest follow.
- **A success chance is never assumed.** Where the recipe book has no success rate the card showed profit at 100%. It now shows an upper bound ("up to X · success chance unknown") until ten crafts of yours say otherwise, and once you have twenty crafts it shows your own rate beside the listed one ("73% listed · 67% yours") and counts with yours.
- **A combo roll credits one craft that worked.** A roll read after a fail, or a second roll for the same craft, counted as another combo won (the Cypri card said 2 won of 1 that worked). Not any more.
- **Crafts the tracker did not see are said, not guessed.** When the queue's count jumps past a craft whose bars were covered, the card and the Crafting tab say "2 crafts not seen" instead of folding them into the rate.
- **What a combo makes, by recipe.** Dishes, potions, powders and materials double; gear credits its upgrade; gear the book has no upgrade for (the Chaser, Dawn, Liberator and Pilgrim bracelets, the Splendent Dragon Lord pieces) keeps its base item and the card says the combo's result isn't known, rather than inventing a second bracelet.
- **The HUD shows what you queue by.** "Sells for X each" ignored a recipe's yield and the combo. The crafting HUD now shows profit a craft and an hour from the same math as the card, and combos won of rolled. Combo rolls are their own feed lines ("combo ×2", "combo: Splendent Orichalcum Longsword") and no longer show as a craft that worked or failed.
- **One number format on the card**, the same short figures as the rest of the window with the full figure on hover, one price line, and "crafts" everywhere instead of "tries" here and "crafts" there.

**Prices**

- **Your own Market reads are checked.** A read whose name is not an item, or whose ask is under a tenth or over three times the board's price, is thrown out instead of standing as your price for a day. Junk names already stored are dropped.
- **The price board comes down with sharing off too.** It is a plain download; only sending your runs needed the sharing switch.
- **Every value is after the Market's cut.** The feed's pickup lines, the Ticker's top drop and the share picture showed the gross figure while the Loot tab showed net. They agree now.
- **Every price says how old it is.** A board price last seen over three days ago is unsure; one over a day old shows dim with its age on hover; a sold average is called that, not "lowest Market price".
- **A typed price gives way after a week** when the board has a sure price, and the card says what you typed and what the board says now.
- **One-listing prices are counted apart**: the Loot tab shows them dimmed with a ~, and the note says how many are not in the total instead of calling them "no price".

**Window**

- **One word for a run.** The Start run tooltip, the first-launch line, the tray and Options all say the same thing: counting happens anyway; a run is what gets saved and shared.
- **Warnings stay on the status line** for 30 seconds and the line wraps, so one you missed while in the game is still there when you tab back.
- **Shortcut keys show on the menu rows** and the tray, and the window's height follows the screen it is on.
- **Tooltips say what the buttons hold**, Kinah footers use the same short format as everything else with the full figure on hover, and the sharing switches sit together in Options.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
