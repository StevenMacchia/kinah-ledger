Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.61: Soul Crystals from kills are all counted, and Cogni has its own name.

- **Kill crystals count while quests complete.** When a quest or reward popped up, the tracker set aside everything that came in for the next 10 seconds as that reward, including the Soul Crystals from your kills. While farming, quests complete every few kills, so crystals kept slipping out. A crystal of 1 or 2 that comes right after a kill now always counts with your kills. A reward's own crystals, like 200 "Special" Soul Crystals, still stay out.
- **Cogni is a pet.** Cogni Soul Crystals were counted with no pet name, so they showed as plain Soul Crystals. They now show as Cogni.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
