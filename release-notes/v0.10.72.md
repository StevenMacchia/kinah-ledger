Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.72: PvP kills and mob kills counted apart in the Abyss.

- **Your kills are told apart.** In the Abyss, the line under the big figure now reads, for example, "9 PvP · 109 mobs" instead of one kill count. Hover it for players and monsters in full.
- **Kills you made count even when the chat scrolled past them.** The game's own messages say which kills were yours, so the kill count, Kinah a kill and drops a kill no longer fall short when chat lines are missed. In a 3-minute Abyss test the chat showed 32 kill payouts; the game's messages had 54 kills.
- **Your deaths are counted too,** and how many of them were to other players, on the same hover.
- Needs Npcap, like the other counts from the game's messages. Without it, kills count from the chat as before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
