Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.7: a ×10 craft is costed with ten times the materials.

- **What a craft cost now follows the recipe you crafted.** Orichalcum Ingot, for one, has a ×1 recipe (2 Ore, 1 Solvent) and a ×10 recipe (20 Ore, 10 Solvent), and the Crafting page, the panel over the game and the last-craft lines priced every try as the ×1 one, so a ×10 run showed a tenth of what it cost. - **The last-craft lines show "×10" and its worth** for those crafts, not the ×1 figures.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
