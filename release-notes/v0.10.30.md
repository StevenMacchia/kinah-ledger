Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.30: a Quests page you can read at a glance.

- **The page starts with the quests that are moving.** The four score tiles are gone: your points are one line at the top of the page once you have some (hover it for this session, today, this week and all time). The empty "Active" card is gone too; this session's quests lead the page as soon as there are any.
- **Points, said once.** The page says what a quest is and how points are earned: reach the next step of something you farm, and bigger steps and rarer materials earn more.
- **Each count shows once.** When today's count or the all-time tally is the same number a shorter quest already shows, that quest is no longer its own row; it is in the shorter quest's tree (click the row).
- **All time shows the two nearest to done.** The rest are behind "N more".
- **Every quest row has the same bar.** A quest under 5% shows an empty bar instead of no bar, so rows no longer jump in height.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
