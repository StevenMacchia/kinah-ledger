Kinah Ledger 1.0.33: in PvP, the DPS meter shows your last 3 kills.

- **Your last 3 PvP kills.** In the Abyss and the arenas, the DPS meter now lists the last 3 enemy players you killed or helped kill, newest first. Each row shows the player's name, whether it was a Kill or an Assist (and who landed the kill), and the damage you did to them.
- **The same breakdown as a boss fight.** Rest the pointer on a row to see your skills on that player: damage, hits, share and your biggest hit for each skill, the same card a boss fight's row opens.
- **Only real numbers.** Damage counts your own hits on that player in the fight that ended with their death. A kill with no hit of yours seen shows no damage rather than a guess. Crit isn't shown for players, because the game doesn't say which hits on players were crits.
- **Works in every meter look.** Kills are coral, assists amber, in each look's own style.
- **Boss fights keep the meter.** A boss pull in the Abyss still shows the DPS meter. To keep the DPS meter in PvP too, turn off Settings › DPS meter › More › PvP kills in PvP.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
