Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.33: a new Events page, a light look that works, and runs counted more accurately.

- **Events page.** One card per place, in the window's own look: the place you're running opens with what just happened and where the run stands, then four figures with labels (an hour, a run, how long a run takes, how many are counted and why the rest aren't), your runs as a table with column names, and the loot as an inventory row. A run left out of the figures says why in its row and opens the fix under it.
- **No more phantom runs.** Your bag, or another window, is never read as a reward screen ("run 23 paid 0" was your bag after leaving the dungeon). Runs start and end where you really enter and leave, so there are no 16-minute runs, and a run you leave before it starts isn't kept. Phantom runs already in your list show as "Not a run" and leave your figures.
- **Expedition Kinah is the reward screen's.** Achievement rewards picked up after a run no longer add to it: an Urugugu Canyon run shows its 600,000.
- **Gear twins named.** Elyos and Asmodian pieces that share one icon are named by your server's faction instead of "unknown gear".
- **Event cards checked against the chat.** A count the chat clearly contradicts is marked not read instead of counted.
- **Accuracy you can read.** The Accuracy chip says "Nothing looked wrong" when nothing was, and shows a number only when something needs a look.
- **A calmer Farming page.** One main rate, the session total labelled, and Accuracy as a small chip that opens the details.
- **Light look.** Buttons, dropdowns, headings, badges and chips are readable in Daylight, and every figure uses one format.
- **Messages stay.** "Saved", "Split into 5 runs" and the like stay on screen for a few seconds.
- **Housekeeping.** The tracker's folder stays a sensible size (crafting pictures capped, old pictures and reports pruned), and its reading log keeps its history instead of starting over.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
