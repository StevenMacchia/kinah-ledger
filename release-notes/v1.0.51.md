Kinah Ledger 1.0.51: ready for the World Market on November 25

- **World Market prices stay apart.** From November 25, Membership users can trade with every server of their faction and region through the World Market and World Exchange. A Market window titled World Market or World Exchange is no longer read as your server's prices: its listings are not shared to the site and do not set the prices the tracker uses. Share Market prices says so instead of sending them.
- **World Market sales are kept apart too.** Chat lines that name the World Market are understood, and they stay out of your server's sale prices and listing times.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
