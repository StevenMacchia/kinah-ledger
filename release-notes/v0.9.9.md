Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.9: one way of writing numbers, the Market fee named, and a few things easier to find.

- **One number format everywhere.** Figures are written in full under 100,000 ("9,500"), then "123.5K", then "1.27M", on the panel, Today, Week, Compare, the tables and the status lines. Before, three formats disagreed (9.5K here, 12,345 there) and 999,600 read as "1000K".
- **The Market fee is said out loud.** The panel and the first tooltips say "after the 12% Market fee". A new switch in Options › General, "Show amounts before the Market fee", shows what things sell for before it instead. Off by default.
- **The shortcut is where you look.** The Start run button's tooltip and a faint hint beside it show the key you set, and the setup check's last card says it too.
- **The tray menu and the setup check follow your look.** In Abyss or Daylight they are no longer Night's colours.
- **Sessions start in plain sight.** The line under the zone adds "counting since 14:05", and a New session button sits beside ⋯ (the same as Clear the tables, with its Undo bar). When the game closes, the status line says the session ended.
- **Where to farm now starts with every region.** The zone list (runs and players per zone) pools all regions by default, and "Your server" is one click away on the card; the choice is remembered. The "Players here" line stays on your server and says "everywhere" when only the pooled figure has a row for the zone. Crafts stay per server.
- **The tray icon is clickable and tells you more.** One click shows or hides the window, and a dot on the icon marks a read problem, paused, or an update ready.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
