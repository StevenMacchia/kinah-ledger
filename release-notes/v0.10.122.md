Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.122: crit and back-attack rates, per skill.

- **Crit and back-attack rates.** Turn on Settings › In game › Parser › Crit and back-attack rates. Each member then gets a crit rate, the share of their damage that came from crits, their biggest crit, and how often they hit from behind. The Parser page shows these figures, and so does the overlay's pop-up when you rest the pointer on a member.
- **Per skill, too.** The pop-up adds crit and back columns beside each skill's share and damage. The Parser page's skill table already had both columns.
- **Off by default while we check them.** The rates are worked out from every hit you see. They are being checked against the game's own Combat Analysis before they come on for everyone. Fights you kept since 0.10.98 already have what the rates need, so turning the switch on fills them in too.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
