Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.122: the open world's DPS is yours, a clean heal chip, crit rates and skill specialties.

- **Your open-world DPS is your own damage.** Around a crowd, such as a field boss, the meter used to give you other players' damage too. One fight read 2.6M DPS when your own hits came to about 24K a second. It now counts exactly your hits, in the numbers the game shows over the monsters. Dungeon and raid figures don't change.
- **"Your DPS" when you're alone.** With no one else on the meter, the foot says Your DPS, not Group DPS.
- **A heal chip instead of the heal line.** A healer's row now shows one green chip after the name with their healing a second. It replaces the small figures under the name and the line along the row. The row keeps its look and height. Rest the pointer on the chip for healing done, healing on others, and how much landed. When the row is too narrow, the chip gives way rather than being cut off.
- **Crit and back-attack rates.** Turn on Settings › In game › Parser › Crit and back-attack rates. Each member then gets a crit rate, the share of their damage from crits, their biggest crit, and how often they hit from behind. Each skill gets its own crit and back columns, on the Parser page and in the overlay's pop-up on a member. This is off by default while the rates are checked against the game's Combat Analysis. Fights kept since 0.10.98 fill in too.
- **Skill specialties.** The Parser page and the pop-up show the specialties each player picked on each skill, for example "Pummel 3·4". This works for everyone in the party, not just you. A pick that doesn't change a skill's hits can't be seen, so a few skills may show fewer picks than were taken.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
