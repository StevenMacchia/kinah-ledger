Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.14: a craft already over when the panel comes into view is counted.

- **The first craft of a queue.** When the crafting panel is found with its queue's first craft already finished, its success bar full, or part-way through its race, that craft was never scored: the tracker only started a race once both bars read empty, so the queue's count passed it and the card said "1 craft not seen". A queue of three Orichalcum Ingot crafts lost its first one that way (2026-11-06 12:40, a critical success that filled the bar at once). Now a bar full at the panel's first sight, or seen climbing to full from it, is kept as a race that ran before the panel was found, and counted the moment the queue's count passes a craft no race was seen for, the same way an instant critical success is. A count that never passes one leaves it out, so the flicker a panel sometimes shows as it opens still counts for nothing.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
