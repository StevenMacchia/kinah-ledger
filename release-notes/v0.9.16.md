Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.16: the game's last three patches (Oct 21, Oct 28 and Nov 4), read into the tracker.

**From the Nov 4 patch**

- **Chaotic Outer Reshanta** is a known zone, so a run there files under its name rather than "Unknown zone". The names of its areas aren't known yet; the tracker reads an unlisted one twice before trusting it, as always.
- **The Abyssal Forge: Ludra rewards are known items**: the Gear Change Vouchers (Ludra and every expedition's), the Abyssal Forge weapon chest, the Abyssal Waves wings, Ludra's artwork, and the Ariel Fragments, Philosopher's Stone: Revelation and Daevanion Crystal: Ariel from the expeditions' Hard modes. They had been counted by name only, with no icon or grade, when they dropped during a run, and not at all outside one.
- **The safe recipes are said on the recipe card.** True Dragon and Star Dragon gear can now be crafted at 100% with no combo and a Bound result. The tracker reads the recipe's name and its Success/Fail bars, not which recipe you picked, so the card for that gear says the safe recipe exists and that its result can't be sold; if every craft works and none rolls a combo, that's the one you used.

**From the Oct 28 patch**

- **The daily gathering limit is counted**: 1,000 gathers a server a day, reset at midnight Pacific. The gathering section shows "N of 1,000 gathers today" with the reset time in your clock, the status line warns at 900, and says so at 1,000, when the game refuses the next gather.
- **The item list is a fresh read** of the dictionary (45 items more), plus the items the last three weeks' events and shops hand out: Soul Crystals, the Lower Abyss Rift Stones, the Instant Clear Tickets, the challenge tickets and the Season 1 Resurrection Spiritstone.

**Not changed**: the Market's cut is still 12%, the chat's wording is the same, and the Expedition Hard and Exploration difficulties aren't told apart (the minimap names the dungeon, not the difficulty), so the Exploration Kina fall-off after 40 runs isn't counted.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
