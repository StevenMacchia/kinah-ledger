Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.5: what each event and expedition dropped for you.

- Click an expedition or event on the Events tab to see everything it dropped across your runs, rarest first, with the most common marked and how many runs each came in.
- Gear from events and expeditions is bound to your character, so it no longer counts toward a run's value. Only the Kinah and the materials you can sell do.
- The crafting window is noted in the log when it opens and closes, so a craft counted wrong can be checked.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
