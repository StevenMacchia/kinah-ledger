Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.11: names read right, and a button for reporting a miscount.

- A name read a letter or two wrong is settled by more than its letters: the colour the game printed it in (each grade's colour is learned from names read exactly) and the loot tables of the enemies you are fighting. A guess from a cut-off line that none of their tables lists waits for the loot list beside your character to confirm it.
- Rows of that loot list whose names are cut off are named by the icon beside them, when one icon is clearly the one shown.
- The chat is enlarged by how tall its text is on your screen, so small text on a 1080p screen reads as well as a 4K one.
- Report a miscount, at the bottom of the window, saves the last minute of what the tracker read and counted to a folder on this PC, for a bug report. Looks wrong? on a drop saves one too. Pictures of the chat are kept only if you say yes (Options › Sharing), since they can show other players' names. Nothing is sent.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
