Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.136: pick an overlay theme on first install.

- **Themes on the welcome screen.** The "Pick your overlay" step of a new install now starts with the overlay's themes: Classic, Wacky, Tactical and Glitch. Pick one to see its looks (Wacky shows Sticker, Neon and Goo), then pick a look. Hover over any look to see it on the preview first. You can still change it any time in Settings › In game.
- **Previewed over the game.** The welcome preview sits on a real game scene, the same one Settings uses, so you can see how each look reads in play.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
