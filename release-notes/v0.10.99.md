Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.99: the Parser's meter can stay on everywhere.

- **Always on, or only boss fights.** The Parser page has a new switch next to the modes: Only boss fights (as before) or Always on. Always on keeps the meter up everywhere, the open world and the Abyss too.
- **It starts from 0 at the right moments.** With Always on, the meter starts from 0 when a boss is pulled, when you enter a Transcendence, Expedition or Sanctuary, and, outside those, after 2 minutes in which nobody in your group fights. Hits from strangers nearby never start it or keep it going. A reconnect or a loading screen back into the same dungeon carries on.
- **A boss's result stays up.** After a kill or a wipe, the meter holds that result until your next hit, which starts a fresh count. Your boss pulls are kept as before, so your bests and Compare work the same.
- **Overlay looks you can see.** Settings › In game › Parser now has one large preview at the overlay's real size, with a button per look. Hover a look to preview it.
- **No impossible hits.** A single misread hit (one Divine Punishment once read 261M in the open world) is left out of every figure, and the fight is marked partial.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
