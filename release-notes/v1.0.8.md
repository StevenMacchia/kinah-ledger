Kinah Ledger 1.0.8: Modern Glass is the default look.

- **Modern Glass by default.** New installs start with the Modern theme's Glass look for the Buddy and the DPS meter. If you never picked a look yourself and are still on Buddy or the standard meter, you move to Glass once. A look you picked stays as it is, and you can switch back any time in Settings.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
