Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.60: cut-off soul rows count as kills, and bound-only rewards are recognized.

- **Cut-off soul rows count as kills.** Every monster drops a soul row ("Soul: Kerubiel (Bound) x1"), and the tracker uses it as proof of a kill. These names are long and scroll in the loot list, so a read often catches only a piece, like "…rubiel (Bound)" or "Tinkerer (Bound)". Those pieces now count as the soul of the monster you're fighting, when they fit only one of their souls. Fewer drops are set aside for lacking a kill behind them, especially when you kill fast.
- **Items that only come bound are recognized.** Some items exist only in a "(Bound)" version, like Sealed Wing Featherdown. The tracker never matched them to its item list, so they weren't counted. It does now, and still treats one as a reward when its "(Bound)" wraps onto the next chat line.
- **Expedition and event runs can be marked verified.** A reward screen's Kinah was counted as if the game's Kinah counter had failed to confirm it, so those runs were never marked verified. Now Kinah the counter confirmed counts as confirmed, and Kinah taken from the reward picture alone is left out of that check.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
