Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.4: crafts stay out of your drops, and every chat line is read three times.

- Crafting: while the crafting window is open, and for a minute after it was last seen, what the loot list beside your character shows is your crafts, never Drops from kills. A long queue whose results arrive after the chat is hidden is counted as crafts too.
- Each new chat line is read up to three times before it counts, and the reading most of them agree on wins. A Kinah amount read as "Kipa" or "46&" is counted right, and a line read twice is counted once. Two pickups a digit apart stay two pickups.
- A new accuracy line under the tables shows how well the count agrees with the game: the Kinah counter at the top of the screen, the kill counter of a quest you are on, and what the loot list caught that the chat missed. It turns orange when a check is more than a tenth off.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
