Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.3: share a session as a picture, compare zones if you want to, and menus that match the app.

- **Share a picture of your farming.** Share, on the Farming card and on the Events page, opens a picture of this session, your last run or this week, ready to paste into Discord. Copy it, save it to Pictures\Kinah Ledger, or copy it as text. Your character name only appears if you switch it on and type it in. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Compare zones and runs, if you want it.** Turn it on in Options › General › Window. Farming then shows your usual and your best in the zone you're in, and the Week side can put two zones (or, on Events, two places) side by side. It's off by default, so the main window stays as it was.
- **No right-click menus.** Everything is a button now, and the ⋯ menu opens in the same style as the rest of the window.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings, goals and history are kept.
