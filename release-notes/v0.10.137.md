Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.137: each overlay theme gets its own skills card and Combat Power by every name, effects are no longer cut off, and the Parser's look saves on its own.

- **A skills card in every theme.** Resting on a member's row in the meter opens their skills card. In the Wacky, Tactical and Glitch themes this used to be the Classic card; now each of the nine looks draws its own (a sticker sheet, an arcade stats screen, candy, a jet's target data, a broadcast player card, a holo readout, a camcorder menu, a corrupted data dump, a terminal profiler). They have every figure the Classic card has: DPS, share, damage, Combat Power, crit and back attacks, up to six skills with their specialties, hits, healing and deaths.
- **Combat Power by every name.** Every themed meter shows each member's Combat Power beside their name, as the Classic meter does. Neon and Goo used to hide it.
- **Effects no longer cut off.** Some effects (a rare drop, a pickup, a kill, a fight starting) ran past the overlay's edge and were cut off. Every effect in every themed look now stays inside the overlay for its whole course.
- **The Parser's look saves on its own.** Picking a new look for the Parser's own window, or changing only a Parser setting, now turns on Save. Before, Save stayed off unless something else changed too.
- **The Parser page fits a small window.** At a small window size, a member's abilities and the group table used to run off the right edge. They now drop their least needed columns to fit, and a skill's SPEC chip is never cut; a long skill name is shortened instead.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
