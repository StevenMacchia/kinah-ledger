Kinah Ledger 1.0.21: much lighter on your PC.

- **Reading the screen is now the backup.** When the tracker reads the game directly and is counting your Kinah and items, it stops reading the loot list off the screen and looks at the rest only every few seconds, so it uses a small share of one core instead of keeping it busy. Big and high-resolution screens benefit most.
- **Nothing is lost.** Inside expeditions and events, with a reward screen open, or whenever the game can't be read directly (no Npcap, or game reading switched off), the screen is read in full as before.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
