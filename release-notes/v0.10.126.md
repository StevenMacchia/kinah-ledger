Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.126: your skills on the meter, with click-through on.

- **Rest the pointer on a name to see their skills.** With click-through on, the meter used to ignore the pointer, so the skill breakdown never opened. Now resting the pointer on a name for half a second opens that player's card beside the meter: each skill's share and damage, their DPS, and crit and specialties when those are on. Move away and it closes. Clicks still go to the game the whole time.
- **The hint says so.** With the pointer over the meter, the line under it now starts with "rest on a name for its skills". It wraps under the meter instead of widening it.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
