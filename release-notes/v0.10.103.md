Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.103: Shugo Festival rewards counted in full.

- **No more checks on Shugo runs.** Every item and amount of an event's rewards is counted, so a Shugo Festival run no longer asks for a check because a reward card's list ran past its window.
- **The right amounts.** Enhance Stone, Centuryroot Token and Abyss Points come in at what the game gave, where the cards often read less (800 Abyss Points where 2,000 came).
- **Bound rewards** are counted as bound, with no value.
- **Kinah stays honest.** A Market sale paid out during an event stays out of the event's run.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
