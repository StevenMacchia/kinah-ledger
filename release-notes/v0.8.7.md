Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.7: restart to update from Options.

- Once a new version has downloaded, Options › Updates shows a Restart now button next to Check now. It installs the update right away, the same as the button on the banner. If you're in the middle of a run, it asks first and saves the run before restarting.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
