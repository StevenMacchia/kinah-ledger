Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.18: reward screens are kept, and event rewards come only from the reward screen.

- The tracker keeps the picture of each expedition and event reward screen for 7 days, so wrong counts can be checked and fixed. Shop and trade screens are the first to be cleared when the folder gets big.
- Event rewards now come only from the reward screen. Until that screen can be read, an event shows "rewards not read" instead of a guess from chat. Its Kinah and kills still count.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
