Kinah Ledger 1.0.25: Ascension Trial boss fights on the Parser.

- **The Ascension Trial's boss fights are kept on the Parser.** Divided Aponos at the end of Nightmare Altar now shows as a boss pull, filed under the Ascension Trial, with your DPS, abilities and the kill time, from your first hit to the kill. Before, those fights were never kept.
- **Sanctum of Loathing's two bosses are one fight.** Featherstorm Duduri and Watchful Raptor Dodori are fought together, so they make one pull under both names. Damage to either counts, their HP adds up, and the pull ends when both are down.
- **Personal bests per difficulty and per Nightmare stage.** A trial's records are kept apart for each difficulty, and a Nightmare boss's for each stage, so Stage 4 and Stage 6 of the same boss never share a best. A Nightmare kill whose stage isn't known yet counts toward no best.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
