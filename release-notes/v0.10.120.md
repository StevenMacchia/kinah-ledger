Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.120: the Parser fixed up, with a skill breakdown on the meter.

- **Hover a row for that player's skills.** Hover any row on the meter to see that player's top skills: each one's share of their damage as a bar, its damage, then hits, heals and deaths. It slides in with its bars growing, and stays current while it's open.
- **Buddy's width on screen.** The Parser's own window now follows the overlay Size setting, the same as the Kinah overlay. At any size it's as wide as Buddy.
- **Drag its edges, click-through or not.** Hover over the left or right edge of the Parser's window and it catches the mouse even with clicks going through to the game, so you can resize it from there. The hover hint says so.
- **Your name, not "Unnamed".** When you fight alone in the open world, your row now shows your character's name. If the tracker hasn't read it yet, it says "You".
- **Self-heals count, potions don't.** Heals from your own skills now count in your heal figures and show the green heal line, even when you're alone. Potions are kept out of every heal figure.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
