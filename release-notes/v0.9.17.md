Kinah Ledger tracker 0.9.17: crafting counted right on a cooking day (Steven's Steamed Cypri and Inina Pasta, Nov 4).

- **A close race is scored once the bars stop.** The fail bar can stop at 98% as the loser while the success bar is still climbing to full; the tracker used to call the race the moment either bar neared full, so a craft that worked was filed as failed (success 83%, fail 98%). A race now counts when both bars have stopped, or the panel goes, by the bar that got full.
- **A combo's second dish is counted from the game's own pop-up.** On a recipe whose combo makes two, the pop-up beside the character says "x2"; that count now credits the second item to the craft, whether the combo's blue race was seen, dropped as too quick, or read as lost. The loot list is read while the panel is up for the queue's own pop-ups alone; nothing else there is a pickup, as before.
- **The chime is the tracker's own.** A watched drop and a price alert used Windows' notify sound, which reads as an error in the app; they now play a short two-note rising chime that belongs to Kinah Ledger (made by `tools/make-chime.js`, no one else's recording). Options' Play previews it at your volume.
- **The panel over the game says it.** A won combo slides in as its own line, "Artisan's Steamed Cypri · combo ×2" with what it added, the head line reads "combo ×2" for a moment, and the rolls count says what a win makes ("Combos ×2 1/3", "Upgrades 1/3"). The Crafting tab's feed already said so; the panel only said "worked".

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
