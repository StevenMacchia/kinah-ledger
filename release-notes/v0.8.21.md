Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.21: expedition rewards come from the loot table on screen.

- An expedition's rewards are now read from its loot table on screen, never from chat: every slot and its count, bound items and currencies too, all shown on the run and sent with it when sharing is on (the site keeps the ones on its item list).
- Anything the tracker can't name shows as "Unknown item" with its count, instead of a guess. The picture of each reward table is kept for 7 days so it can be checked.
- The table is read again until it has settled, so a table still fading in no longer gives wrong items.
- Gear for other classes and look-alike items are told apart by grade and by the table's own sets (Divine Canyon among Divine Canyon pieces).
- A Transcendence is its own kind of run, also counted from its loot table.
- Events still show "rewards not read" until their reward screen can be read.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
