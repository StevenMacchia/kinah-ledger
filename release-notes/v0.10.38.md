Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.38: taking things out of storage is never a drop.

- **Storage withdrawals stay out of your loot.** An item taken out of storage right after putting something in counted as a drop, because the tracker took "Retrieved item(s) from Storage." for the "Stored item(s) in Storage." line still on screen. The same happened when you'd taken something out earlier in a long session. Both are fixed: what you take out is set aside, and drops after it count as usual.
- **Kinah taken out of storage isn't income.** It no longer shows up as farmed Kinah or as a reward.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
