Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.39: gear from expeditions and dungeons gets its own row, worth nothing.

- **Run gear sits apart from the loot.** Gear from an expedition, dungeon or event is bound to you and can't be sold, so it never added to the value. It now has its own row under Paid out and under each run, "19 gear · bound, no value", with each piece and how many you got, instead of sitting among the loot.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
