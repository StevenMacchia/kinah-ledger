Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.113: total damage, Avg. DPS and the TOP DPS plate.

- **Total and group DPS.** The meter's foot shows the fight's total damage beside the group's DPS.
- **Avg. DPS.** When a fight is over (a few seconds without fighting in the open world, or a boss kill or wipe), a green AVG. DPS badge marks the held figure: the fight's average.
- **TOP DPS.** When a boss fight in a dungeon ends, a small gold TOP DPS plate sits on the row of whoever did the most damage.
