Kinah Ledger 1.0.18: one place for settings, copying made obvious, and the last fight stays up.

- **Settings live in Settings.** The Parser page no longer carries switches; every DPS meter option has one row in Settings › DPS meter, and new installs start with the meter's switches on (Always on, Click-through, Expanded and more).
- **Copy is front and centre.** A big Copy picture button and Copy text at the top of the Parser page, Ctrl+C to copy the fight as a picture and Ctrl+Shift+C as text, and a "killed in 2:19 · Copy picture" prompt after every boss.
- **The last fight stays up.** After a boss fight the Parser page keeps it, graphs and all, until the next boss is pulled; older pulls below still open with a click and a clear Back.
- **Terminal types.** In the Terminal look, pickups, rare drops, new bests and clears type out character by character.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
