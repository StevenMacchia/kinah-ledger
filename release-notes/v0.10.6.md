Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.6: the Expeditions and events page lists every run in a box that scrolls.

- **Every run, one box.** ALL RUNS sits at the top of the page: each run on its line (when it started, the place, how long it took, what it made) with its Kinah and every reward as slots under it, each with its card on hover. The box scrolls past 440 px instead of the page growing. A dropdown at its top picks the order: newest first, best first, by place, or longest first.
- **The places fold.** The card for each place sits under the box folded to one line (its runs, what they made, when it was last run); the place being run, or the last one run, stays open. A click opens or folds a place; a click on a run in the box opens its place with that run's sheet.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
