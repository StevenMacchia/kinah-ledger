Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.6: the Events page's TODAY strip shows every run, in a box that scrolls.

- **A run at a time.** The strip lists each of today's expeditions and events, newest first: when it started, its name, how long it took, what it made, and its Kinah and every reward as slots under it. The box scrolls instead of growing down the page. A run left out of the figures says so.
- **A dropdown instead of links.** "Each run" at the strip's top opens a menu: a run at a time, or every run's rewards together by worth, amount or quality. LOOT on the Farming page has the same dropdown, "Sort: worth", in place of the word links.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
