Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.17: the Expeditions and events page is one box.

- **One box.** The TODAY strip stays at the top. Under it there is now a single box: pick the expedition or event from the dropdown in its band, and it shows what you got there, laid out like the TODAY strip (the Kinah, then the rewards in slots, sorted by worth, amount or quality, two rows and Show all), with one line of figures above (an hour, a run, how long a run, the best run). This session or All time switches what the box covers. The four big figures, the Materials, Currencies and Gear rows, the ALL RUNS box and the list of other places are gone from the page.
- **The rest, under ⋯.** Share the last run, Show every run (the ALL RUNS box, each run on its line with what it gave, as a second box), Save as CSV and Clear the runs shown. A run with something to look at shows as one line in the box; a click opens its sheet with the fix.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
