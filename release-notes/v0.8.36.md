Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.36: a window that stays quick as the session grows, and Enhance Stone never credited to a kill.

- **Quick to click and drag.** Once a farming session had rows in it, the window rebuilt every row and card twice a second, and clicks and drags waited behind that. Now the tables and cards only take new words when something changes, and dragging the window no longer fights its sizing. Counting is untouched.
- **Enhance Stone.** It comes from expedition reward screens, never from a kill, but the shipped loot tables listed it on 14 open-world enemies. Those entries are ignored now, so a stone can't be credited to an enemy that never dropped it.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
