Kinah Ledger tracker 0.8.36: what you gather inside an expedition is the run's reward.

- **Gathering inside an expedition.** Odyle and other materials you gather inside an expedition or a Transcendence now count as that run's rewards, on top of its reward table, and show and upload with the run. They no longer go to Gathering. Events are unchanged: their rewards never count as gathering.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background. Settings and history are kept.
