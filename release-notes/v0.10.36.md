Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.36: click any craft for its recipe, and the game's own art.

- **Click anything on the Crafting page.** Best crafts today, Next up, Just crafted, the recipe cards and the Full view table all open a recipe window: how to craft it (the materials and what each costs), the profit a craft and an hour, what it sells for, and a Market section with the other servers' price and how sure the costs are. Drag it anywhere, beside the game if you like. It stays open while you look around, and the next click puts the new recipe in it. Esc closes it. - **Calmer, clearer.** The page you're on is lit quietly instead of in solid white, sentences are easier to read, cards have a faint top edge, the main button has a soft gold glow, and pages fade in when you switch.
