Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.138: your boss bests on your kinahledger.com profile, if you turn them on.

- **Your boss bests on your profile, if you want them.** A new switch in Options › Sharing, **Show my boss bests on my kinahledger.com profile**, is off unless you turn it on. Nothing about your fights leaves your PC unless you do. On, your own best kill on each boss (your DPS, the fight's length and date) and your latest pull of it show in the Boss DPS tab of your signed-in profile at kinahledger.com, per character, for you alone. Only your own numbers go: your group's names and DPS stay on your PC, and nothing is ranked. Turn it off and the site forgets them.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
