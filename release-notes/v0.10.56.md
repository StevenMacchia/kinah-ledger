Kinah Ledger tracker 0.10.56: the tracker comes alive. Big moments get a moment, the window answers the mouse, and your week makes a picture worth sharing.

- **A rare drop is a moment.** When a Legend, Unique or Epic item drops (or one you watch), the window shows it for a second and a half: its tile lands in its grade's frame with a burst ring and a sweep of light, with the name and what it sells for, then it lifts away and the page is as it was. The overlay's rare card on Buddy and Pop makes the same entrance. Several at once show as one, the best with "+2 more".
- **Your best moments get a moment.** When this hour passes your best in the zone while you play, a gold light crosses the big figure, a "★ New best" tag appears for a few seconds and the ring pops. Each time today's Kinah passes 1M, 2.5M, 5M, 10M or more, the light crosses again with a "1M Kinah today" tag. It happens only as it happens: never on starting the tracker, changing zone or character, or a new day.
- **The run-end recap comes alive.** The card rises into place, its Kinah, time and loot count up one after another, your drops land one by one with the best glowing in its colour, and a gold "New best here" tag sweeps in on a record. With Compare zones and runs on, and three earlier runs there, a line says how this run stood against your average there ("+18% vs your average here").
- **Week wrapped and Day wrapped.** A recap of your week or today for the current character: total made, time and Kinah an hour, top zone, best run, your biggest drop glowing in its grade, crafting profit, and how you did against last week. It plays in, then copies or saves as a picture to share. The buttons sit under the bag's Week and Today sides and in the Events TODAY strip.
- **The rate chart is live.** When a new minute lands, the line runs out to it and the scale eases to fit, the newest point pops once, the fill under the line takes your look's gold, and hovering the chart reads any minute's time and rate.
- **The window answers the mouse.** The page tab's light slides to the tab you pick and the page cross-fades as it rises in; switches slide their pill; cards and tiles lift under the pointer, an item tile catches a sweep of light, and buttons press in and spring back. A lit switch also keeps its light while the mouse is on it.
- **Badge moves like the others.** The default overlay comes alive on every pickup: its figures roll up (the hourly ones flashing green), its edge glows gold, the ring of your best here sweeps to its new share and pulses once when you pass your best, the live dot flashes in the item's grade colour, and a rare drop beats its frame. Notices and run lines fade instead of snapping.
- **Overlay figures move only for a pickup.** On Pop and Buddy too, a price refresh or loading another session no longer rolls figures or lights the edge.

Every effect plays once, on a change, and nothing loops, so the tracker costs the game nothing while it sits still. All of it is off when Windows animations are off.

Installed copies update by themselves in the background.
