## Rules Steven set today (keep them)

- The game's lists are whitelists. Items: `data/tw-items.json` (built into the exe; the site has the IDs and names in
  `backend/src/item-ids.ts`, generated by `scripts/gen-item-ids.mjs`). Zones: `data/zones.txt` (about 330 names, built
  into the exe; copied to the site's `data/zones.txt`, generated into `backend/src/zones.ts` by `scripts/gen-zones.mjs`).
  Pets: `ChatParser.KnownPets` and `KNOWN_PETS` in `backend/src/lists.ts`. A name not on a list is never counted and never
  sent; the site drops it at intake too (`validate.ts`, `dropped` in the response). Another site session once loosened
  this ("looks like a place", "looks like a name"); Steven said no. Keep both copies of each list in step.
- Soul Points never count as anything but a kill. Soul Crystals are their own section under Drops, no drop %.
- Farming time comes from the chat: a loot, Kinah, kill, gather, Soul Crystal or Soul Point line keeps the clock
  running, 60 s with none stops it. Per-hour rates divide by it (`SessionEngine.Active`, zone `Active`); counts and
  drop rates don't care. The payload's `minutes` and zone minutes are farming minutes.
- One window, Drops and Gathered stacked, each folds on its header. Price column on every row (lowest Market price;
  dashes until the site has prices for his server, Siel (Elyos): no snapshot exists yet; another session is building
  reference prices from a2-tracker.com and aion2.market in the site repo's `ref-prices` worktree).
- Overlay (`Ui/HudWindow.cs`): one short wide strip, three figures (Kinah/hr, drops value/hr, total estimated value),
  opacity slider in Settings, shown whenever its switch is on, steps aside for the zone read when it overlaps the minimap corner.
- Zone: read on its own clock, every 5 s while none is known, forever; every 10 s otherwise.
- Tab in and out must never break reading: the chat area follows the game window (`TrackerSession.FollowGameAsync`).
- Copy: short, plain words. Commit messages explain the why in a paragraph. No pushes, no releases unless Steven says.
